## Personalization check (Q14 × lowest dimension × profile, and fallbacks)
- **Two signals disagree.** The results card's «فرصتك الجاية» uses `r.primary` (Q13 can override it), but the landing headline uses `lowestDim = dims.indexOf(min)`. A mother can be told her opportunity is "تخصيص" and then get a "الأنماط" headline. Ties always resolve to dimension 0.
- **The problem section ignores her situation.** It is hard-coded to sleep: «تخيّلي إن وقت النوم يتكرر فيه نفس الموقف كل ليلة.» So a mother who chose food or anger gets a personalized headline, then the sleep story one scroll later.
- **Q14 = G (free text)** falls to `_generic`. Her own words, and her Q15 answer (the most personal input there is), are never used on the landing.
- **`?plan`** works and the headline is fine («بدل ما تجربي نصيحة جديدة كل يوم، ابدئي بخطة تناسب طفلك»), but the subhead is grammatically broken (see item 3).
- **`?focus=` is not supported at all**, so TikTok and email links cannot deep-link a personalized landing.

## Results page / invite card notes
- The reciprocity is genuine. The results give real value whether or not she joins: strength, opportunity, a situation-specific first step, her own Q15 words reflected back, and «من فين تبدأي؟». Keep that.
- The invite card headline «عرفتي نقطة البداية. ايش الخطوة الجاية؟» is good. The body is six stacked paragraphs that never say what she would get, what it costs, or that it is paid guidance from a real person, so the button is a mild leap.
- The profile title appears twice back to back (as the h1, then again as `.lvl` inside the ring card).

## Biggest leaks
1. **Invented scarcity.** «الدفعة الأولى تستقبل ١٠ أمهات فقط» is shown twice, but the brief says the number is not confirmed. It is the single most trust-damaging line for this audience and brand, and it breaks the brief's "honest scarcity only" rule.
2. **Personalization breaks after the headline.** It uses a different dimension from the result she just read (`lowestDim` vs `primary`), the problem story is hard-coded to bedtime, Q15 and the G free text are never used, and there is no `?focus=`. Several headline variants are 5-line, formulaic or vague, so the hero's 5-second clarity depends on which cell she lands in.
3. **The offer's real risk-reducers and next steps are missing.** "Monthly, cancel anytime" is never stated, nothing says what happens after the Google Form, and the person behind «أنا والقروب معاك» is never introduced. Meanwhile the CTA appears in the hero before the price and inclusions.
4. **Arabic voice drifts and the page is too long.** It shifts into MSA in the FAQ («ماذا لو», «لا توجد», «يمكن أن تكون»), has a dozen typos, and the `?plan` subhead is broken. All of that sits on a 14-screen landing that restates "every two weeks" 14 times and puts the strongest proof below the price.
