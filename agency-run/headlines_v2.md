# Headlines v2 (Gate 2 redo): مدار البيت | الخطة الحية

Specialist 8, Direct Response Copywriter. This replaces `headlines.md`, which the client rejected. Strategy is unchanged (positioning, belief, offer and market are approved). Only the wording is new.

## What changed from v1

| v1 failure (client, brief §11) | v2 rule applied to every line |
|---|---|
| Literal translation («نفس الغضب يتكرر كل يوم؟») | Each line was written from scratch in Saudi Arabic and read aloud. The English is a back-translation, not the source. |
| Stitched halves (her scene plus a method clause) | One thought per line. If there is a second clause, it answers the first in the same voice («...؟ أنا معاكي فيها.»), or the line is a single sentence. No abstract method words like «نشوف ايش يسبقها». |
| Vague | Every line names a concrete moment: the iPad, the عزيمة, three foods, «طنّشيه», «باقي خمس دقايق». |
| Lukewarm | Charged words she would use herself: معركة، همّ، قلبك يتقطع، على أعصابك، تقوم القيامة، ينقلب البيت. |
| Third person, team voice | No «مع أنهار», no company "we". Anhar says «أنا» and «معاكي», or the line speaks straight to her. |
| Language file | شي، أنتي، معاكي، ايش، فين، تبدأي (as in the locked table). No extra ن, no tanween fath, no em dash, no ellipsis. |

Promises are about her only: she won't face it alone, she understands what keeps repeating, she knows where to start, the plan starts from his day, and it gets reviewed with her every two weeks. Nothing is promised about how the child sleeps, eats, talks or calms down.

## d1: المعركة باسمها، وأنا معاكي فيها (Her battle, named raw, and I'm in it with her)

**Rationale:** I name her daily battle the way she would say it herself, and then, in the same breath, I answer as «أنا»: she won't carry it alone. This is Extreme Love in first person. The promise is true: the plan is reviewed with her every two weeks, and the group is there in between.

**Sub (27 words):** أنا أنهار، والخطة الحية متابعة شهرية معاي. أشوف يوم طفلك كامل، أبني معاكي خطة تركز على مجال واحد، وأراجعها معاكي كل أسبوعين عشان ما تبدأي من الصفر.
*I'm Anhar, and The Living Plan is monthly, personal follow-up with me. I look at your child's whole day, build a plan with you that focuses on one area, and review it with you every two weeks so you don't start from zero.*

| | Headline | Back-translation | What she gets in 5 seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| A النوم | كل ليلة نفس المعركة وقت النوم؟ أنا معاكي فيها. | Every night, the same battle at bedtime? I'm in it with you. | Bedtime is a fight every night, and she's saying she'll be in it with me. |
| B الخروج والزيارات | كل عزيمة صارت همّ وتحسبي لها ألف حساب؟ خليني أحسب حسابها معاكي. | Every family gathering has become a worry you plan a thousand things for? Let me do that planning with you. | She knows how much I dread gatherings, and she'll prepare for them with me. |
| C التواصل | يبغى يقول لك شي وما يقدر، وقلبك يتقطع؟ خليني أفهمه معاكي. | He wants to tell you something and can't, and your heart breaks? Let me understand him with you. | She knows how much it hurts when he can't tell me, and she'll work on understanding him with me. |
| D الأكل | نفس الثلاث أكلات كل يوم، وخوفك عليه يكبر؟ خليني أشيله معاكي. | The same three foods every day, and your worry for him keeps growing? Let me carry it with you. | She sees how worried I am about his eating, and I won't carry that alone. |
| E الانتقال | كل ما تطفي الآيباد تبدأ معركة؟ خليني أوقف جنبك فيها. | Every time you turn off the iPad, a battle starts? Let me stand by you in it. | Turning off the iPad is a fight every time, and she'll stand with me in it. |
| F الغضب | يومك كله على أعصابك، تستني متى يعصّب؟ أنا معاكي فيه. | Your whole day on edge, waiting for when he'll get angry? I'm in it with you. | She knows I spend the day bracing for the next outburst, and she's offering to be with me in it. |
| Default (G, ?plan) | نفس المواقف ترجع كل يوم، وأنتي تواجهيها لحالك؟ خلاص، أنا معاكي. | The same situations come back every day, and you face them alone? That's it, I'm with you. | The same things keep happening and I'm alone with them. Now someone is with me. |

## d2: افهمي ليش، واعرفي من فين تبدأي (Understand it, and know your next step)

**Rationale:** This is the Mindvalley pattern in Saudi Arabic. Each line is a bold, outcome-led promise about what she gets (she understands what keeps repeating and knows where to start), never about what the child does. It builds on the client's own sentence from the language file: «افهمي ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك ومن فين تبدأي».

**Sub (28 words):** أنا أنهار، وهذي الخطة الحية، متابعة شهرية معاي: أشوف يوم طفلك كامل، أختار معاكي أول مجال تبدأي فيه، وأراجع الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين عشان تعرفي دايم خطوتك الجاية.
*I'm Anhar, and this is The Living Plan, monthly follow-up with me: I look at your child's whole day, choose with you the first area to start on, and review the plan with you every two weeks so you always know your next step.*

| | Headline | Back-translation | What she gets in 5 seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| A النوم | افهمي ليش وقت النوم صار معركة كل ليلة، واعرفي من فين تبدأي. | Understand why bedtime has become a battle every night, and know where to start. | I'll finally understand why bedtime is a fight, and I'll know where to begin. |
| B الخروج والزيارات | افهمي ليش كل عزيمة صارت همّ، واعرفي كيف تجهزي لها. | Understand why every family gathering has become a worry, and know how to prepare for it. | I'll understand why gatherings are so hard and know how to get ready for the next one. |
| C التواصل | افهمي ايش يحاول يقول لك، بدل ما تخمّني طول اليوم. | Understand what he's trying to tell you, instead of guessing all day. | No more guessing all day. I'll work out what he's trying to tell me. |
| D الأكل | افهمي ليش كل لقمة جديدة صارت معركة، وابدئي معاه بدون ضغط. | Understand why every new bite has become a battle, and start with him without pressure. | I'll understand the fight over food, and I can start new foods without forcing him. |
| E الانتقال | افهمي ليش كل ما تطفي الآيباد ينقلب البيت، واعرفي ايش تسوي. | Understand why the house turns upside down every time you turn off the iPad, and know what to do. | I'll understand why turning off the iPad blows up, and I'll know what to do about it. |
| F الغضب | افهمي ايش يصير في يومه قبل ما ينفجر، واعرفي من فين تبدأي. | Understand what happens in his day before he explodes, and know where to start. | Something happens before the outburst. I'll see it and know where to begin. |
| Default (G, ?plan) | افهمي ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك، واعرفي من فين تبدأي. | Understand why the same situations keep repeating with your child, and know where to start. | I'll understand what keeps repeating and know where to start. |

## d3: ضد النصيحة الجاهزة (Against the ready-made advice)

**Rationale:** Love-or-hate. Each line names the exact advice she has been given for her situation (cut down on visits, every child talks in his own time, let him get hungry, five more minutes, ignore him) and takes her side against it. The stance is against generic advice. It is never against her, her family or his specialists.

**Sub (25 words):** أنا أنهار، وما أعطيكي نصائح جاهزة: في الخطة الحية أبني معاكي خطة من يوم طفلك هو، مجال واحد في كل مرة، وأراجعها معاكي كل أسبوعين.
*I'm Anhar, and I don't hand you ready-made tips: in The Living Plan I build a plan with you from your own child's day, one area at a time, and review it with you every two weeks.*

| | Headline | Back-translation | What she gets in 5 seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| A النوم | جربتي كل نصائح النوم ولسه نفس المعركة؟ لأنها ما تعرف طفلك. | You've tried every sleep tip and it's still the same battle? Because they don't know your child. | The tips failed because they weren't about my child, not because of me. |
| B الخروج والزيارات | قالوا لك قللي الطلعات والزيارات؟ أنتي تستاهلي تطلعي، وأنا أجهزها معاكي. | They told you to cut down on outings and visits? You deserve to go out, and I'll prepare for them with you. | I don't have to stay home. She'll help me get ready to go out. |
| C التواصل | قالوا لك كل طفل يتكلم في وقته؟ وأنتي اليوم تبغي تفهميه. | They told you every child talks in his own time? But you want to understand him today. | Someone finally gets that I can't just wait. I want to understand him now. |
| D الأكل | قالوا لك خليه يجوع وبياكل؟ أنا أبدأ معاكي من الأكل اللي ياكله. | They told you let him get hungry and he'll eat? I start with you from the food he already eats. | No forcing. She starts from the foods he already accepts. |
| E الانتقال | تقولي له باقي خمس دقايق، ولسه كل ما تطفي الآيباد تقوم القيامة؟ | You tell him "five more minutes", and still, every time you turn off the iPad, all hell breaks loose? | That's exactly my evening. The five-minute warning doesn't work for him. |

## Recommendation: d1, المعركة باسمها، وأنا معاكي فيها

Why d1:
1. **It meets each of the client's five objections head-on.** d1 is the only direction with Anhar's «أنا» inside the headline itself. It uses the client's own words for the pain («كل ليلة نفس المعركة»). Every line is her battle followed by Anhar's personal reply, so it reads as one person talking. Nothing is a scene with a method clause glued on.
2. **The promise is true and about her.** "I'm in it with you" is what the service actually delivers: a review with her every two weeks, plus the group in between. It promises nothing about the child.
3. **The sub does the Mindvalley job.** The headline makes her feel it. The sub says who (أنا أنهار), what (monthly follow-up), how (whole day, one area, every two weeks) and which pain goes away (starting from zero).

Use of the others: **d2** is the clearest and calmest. Its default line is the client's own sentence, so it is the best alternative for the `?plan` email link if she wants a less emotional entry. **d3** has the sharpest lines for TikTok hooks and ads, especially C and F.

### d1 sub by lowest dimension (headline stays fixed per situation)
