# The Living Plan "curriculum": what I need from you

You can answer in English, Arabic or a voice-note transcript. Rough notes are fine, and I'll turn them into copy. Only your real experience goes on the page, so where you don't know something, write "not sure" and I'll leave it out.

---

## Part 1: For each focus area (do this 7 times)

The 7 areas are: Bedtime · Outings & visits · Communication · Food · Transitions / iPad · Anger & outbursts · General (no specific area).

**1. Directions of growth.**
When you work on this area, what are the 3–5 directions you usually work toward?
- Describe what you'd actually see in their day if things move in that direction. Concrete, observable things, not feelings.
- Example of the level of detail I need, for bedtime: "bedtime follows the same few steps every night", "he falls asleep in his own bed", "fewer wake-ups in the night". Use your real ones.

**2. Your usual order.**
What do you usually work on first, second and third? For example: first we figure out X, then we set up Y, then we build Z.
This becomes "Weeks 1–2 / Weeks 3–4 / Weeks 5–6".

**3. Typical length.**
How many two-week cycles does this area usually take before it's part of their life? (2? 3? 6?)

**4. What a mother actually DOES in a cycle.**
Give 2–3 real examples of steps from past plans in this area. For example: a picture schedule, a sentence to say before turning off the iPad, a change to the evening order, a small game during dinner.
These make the page tangible.

**5. A real before/after from your work** (anonymous, no names).
What was happening, what changed, and over how many weeks? Only things that really happened.

**6. Anything you'd never promise in this area.**

## Part 2: The service itself (answer once)

1. **From subscribing to her first plan.** How long does it take: the onboarding file, the two questionnaires, the child profile?
2. **What's inside one plan.** What does she receive? How many steps? Is there anything she can print or use, like cards, a tracking sheet, or sentences to say? About how many minutes a day does it take?
3. **The check-in.** What does it look like? Written questions? Voice notes? How long until she gets the next plan?
4. **Between check-ins.** Can she message you with a question? If yes, how?
5. **Numbers we can honestly show.** How many mothers or children have you worked with? For how many years? Do you have a background or training you want mentioned?
6. **Proof.** Can you get permission to quote 2–3 more mothers, even one line each?
7. **The term.** Do you want "direction of growth" shown on the page in Arabic? «اتجاه النمو»? «وين رايحين»? Or another term you use?
8. **Real urgency.** Is there a real opening date for the first group? A bonus that's only for the first group? (The Vishen transcript example: "the class starts Friday" is real urgency.)
