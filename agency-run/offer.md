## 3. Why it's worth paying for

The value is **continuity and focus**, not more content. Make it concrete against the three things she has already tried (positioning §1, research competitors):

- **Free tips and articles** (webteb, mawdoo3, Instagram, forum programs): free, but they don't know her child and never come back to check. → We give one priority and the next step, and we come back every two weeks.
- **Sessions** (home sessions ~150 ريال each; Khamsat consults $5–20; centres far more): the specialist works with the child for an hour. → We work with **her**, on the rest of his day at home, alongside those sessions. Not a cheaper session; a different job.
- **One-off consults and fixed programs:** a single answer or a plan that never changes. → The plan changes with his response, including hard days and setbacks.

**The one value sentence for the price block (AR):**
«مو اشتراك في نصايح. هي متابعة شهرية مع أحد يعرف قصة طفلك، يشتغل معاكي على مجال واحد، ويرجع يراجع الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين عشان ما تبدئي من الصفر.»

**Decisions:**
- Do **not** compare the price to session prices or to "100 ألف ريال سنويًا" for centres. It invites the wrong comparison (she'll do per-session math) and positions us against specialists.
- Do **not** break the price into "per day". Calm brand, no hype math.
- Do say the price plainly and once in the price block, and once more only inside the merged price objection.

## 4. Expectations to set (what it is and isn't)

Say these plainly, close to the price/CTA, in her dialect (the live FAQ slips into MSA here; rewrite):

- **It is:** personal monthly guidance with Anhar; one area at a time; a review and adjustment every two weeks; works inside your existing routine; alongside his specialists.
- **It isn't:** therapy, a diagnosis, a replacement for speech therapy/OT/specialists, a cure, a ready-made program, a library of tips.
- **No guaranteed result:** «ما نقدر نوعدك بنتيجة معينة لطفلك. اللي نوعدك فيه هو الطريقة: نبدأ من يومه، نشتغل على شي واحد، ونراجع ونعدّل معاكي كل أسبوعين.»
- **Alongside therapy:** «الخطة الحية مو بديل عن جلسات التخاطب أو العلاج أو المختصين. هي اللي تربط اللي يتعلمه هناك بيومه في البيت.» (replaces the live «ويمكن أن تكون مكملة»)
- **Safety line (focus F only, from positioning):** if he hurts himself, point her to a doctor or specialist; never imply the plan handles that alone.
- **Who it's for / not for** (belief.md, locked): «إذا تدوري علاج يشيل التوحد أو نتيجة مضمونة، هذي الخطة مو المكان المناسب لك. وإذا تدوري أحد يفهم يوم طفلك معاكي ويعدّل الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين، هذا اللي نسويه.»
- **The results stay hers:** nothing on the results page or invite card implies she must join to keep or use her result.

## 5. Objections to handle

The live page has 6 cards + a 10-item FAQ, with money and price as two cards making the same argument (audit item 7), and the offer re-explained in every card. Final structure: **6 cards** (each answers its doubt once, short, and does not re-list the inclusions) + **a short FAQ** for factual questions.

### Cards (in this order)

1. **«عندي نصايح كثير، ليش أدفع؟» / isn't this free advice?** (new; research: generic content dominates)
   Answer: She's right, tips are everywhere. That's not what's missing. Tips don't know her child, don't pick one thing, and don't come back two weeks later. Close on the locked core line: «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي طريقة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم.»

2. **«ما عندي وقت»**
   Keep the live idea (no worksheets; steps sit inside snack time, play, the routine that already repeats). Add the decided answer: one area only, so she isn't asked to change everything. Cut the repeated "every two weeks" paragraph. End: «مو مطلوب منك تفضّي حياتك عشان تبدئي.»

3. **«جربت قبل وما نفع» / «طيب… ولو ما ضبط؟»** (covers "how is this different" + "will it work for my child")
   Answer: no promise every step works the first time, and no promise of a result. The difference: she isn't left to carry on with something that doesn't fit; every two weeks we look at what happened and change it. Case 1 quote may sit here or just above, labelled as one mother's experience, not a typical result.

4. **«٧٤٩ ريال مو مبلغ بسيط»** (MERGE of «الفترة هذي نحاول نرتّب مصاريفنا» + «السعر مبالغ فيه»)
   Answer, in this order: acknowledge it honestly («مو مبلغ بسيط، ومن حقك تعرفي ايش تاخذي مقابله»); one sentence of what it buys (§3 value sentence); then the risk reducer: «اشتراك شهري، وتقدري تلغي في أي وقت.» No comparison to session prices, no per-day math, no guilt.

5. **«هل أنا السبب؟»**
   Keep the live frame: «تكرار الموقف مو دليل إنك أم مقصّرة.» Add belief 2: «اللي تشوفيه كل يوم مو تفاصيل جانبية. هو أهم معلومة عن طفلك.» Fix the typos.

6. **«أحتاج أكلم زوجي قبل ما أقرر»**
   Keep the live idea (talk about concrete steps, not who's right). Add one practical line: it's monthly and she can cancel any time, so it's not a long commitment to argue over. Fix «تعدّلو», «تكسبِي».

## 6. How to say the value without promising outcomes

**The framing line (use in the price block and as the backbone of card 3):**
«نوعدك بالطريقة، مو بالنتيجة: نبدأ من يوم طفلك كامل، نشتغل على مجال واحد، ونراجع الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين.»

Rules:
- Promise the **process** (whole day, one area, review every two weeks, someone who knows his story) and **her clarity** (she knows the priority and the next step). Never his outcome (sleeps, calms, talks, eats).
- Outcomes appear only inside owner case stories, verbatim, labelled «تجربة أم وحدة».
- Banned in the offer section: «نتائج مضمونة»، «علاج»، «يتخلص من»، «ينام أسرع»، «يهدأ»، «يبدأ يتكلم»، «الأول من نوعه»، «أفضل».
