# Brand belief system (this page): مدار البيت | الخطة الحية (Specialist 5)

Built on: brief.md (incl. §9 Gate 1 approval), positioning.md (approved: "Whole day. One area. Every two weeks."), research.md and market.md for context.
Purpose: give the post-assessment pages (result card, invite card, landing) a point of view a mother can love, and that the wrong mother can leave without being pushed out. Conviction, not provocation.

---

## The belief statement (the spine)

**EN:** Your child grows and learns in his real day, at home, and the person who sees that day is you. So we build the plan from what you see, work on one thing at a time, and adjust it with you as he responds, alongside his specialists, not instead of them.

**AR:** طفلك يتعلم ويكبر في يومه الحقيقي في البيت، واللي يشوف هذا اليوم هو أنتي. عشان كذا نبني الخطة من اللي تشوفيه، نشتغل على شي واحد، ونعدّلها معاكي حسب استجابته، مع جلساته مو بدالها.

This is the positioning's mechanism (whole day, one area, every two weeks) said as a belief instead of a feature. Every belief below is one side of it.

---

## Beliefs we say out loud

1. **The home is where his day actually happens.** The session is an hour; the rest of the day is at home, with her. Growth has to enter that day.
   - AR line: «الجلسات مهمة، بس أغلب يوم طفلك يصير في البيت. وهناك لازم تعيش الخطة.»
   - Attracts: the mother who leaves every session thinking "and now what do I do at home?" (research: "who was there with an everyday plan to put it together?").
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants to hand the child to an expert and have it handled outside the home. She needs a centre, not us.
   - Guardrail: never against therapy. "Alongside" every time.

2. **What she sees every day is real information, not background detail.** Her observations are the most valuable input the plan has; the intake and reviews are built on them.
   - AR line: «اللي تشوفيه كل يوم مو تفاصيل جانبية. هو أهم معلومة عن طفلك.»
   - Attracts: the mother who has been rushed in appointments, or told "this is normal" without being asked what happens before the moment.
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants a verdict without being asked anything. We will ask questions first (case 3: extra questions before anything else).
   - Replaces «الأم مفتاح النجاح» (which reads as pressure/blame) with respect for what she knows.

3. **Autism isn't something to fix. We understand him and make his day work better for him and for her.** No cure language, no "getting rid of" anything.
   - AR line: «ما نحاول نغيّر طفلك. نحاول نفهم يومه معاكي، ونختار أول شي نشتغل عليه.»
   - Attracts: the mother who loves her child as he is and is tired of programs that treat him as a problem to remove.
   - Gently repels: the mother looking for a cure, a diet that "heals", or a guaranteed result (the عالم حواء "شفاء/علاج" threads). The page says plainly this isn't for her (client approved at Gate 1).

4. **A method that worked for another child is not automatically right for yours.** That's why the plan starts from his whole day, not from a list.
   - AR line: «الطريقة اللي نفعت مع طفل ثاني مو بالضرورة تناسب طفلك. عشان كذا نبدأ من يومه هو.»
   - Attracts: the mother with a phone full of saved tips and screenshots that "didn't work with him".
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants a ready-made program to apply as-is (she'll be happier with a fixed course).
   - Never use the filler «كل طفل مختلف» on its own; always follow it with what we do because of it.

5. **One priority at a time beats ten tips a week.** She deserves to know the next step, not carry everything at once.
   - AR line: «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي طريقة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم.» (locked core message)
   - Attracts: the overwhelmed mother who has tried "enough" and is exhausted by information.
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants to fix sleep, food, outings and anger all this month. We'll say: one area, then the next.

6. **A plan that never changes stops fitting him.** Hard days, setbacks and routine changes are part of the plan, not a failure of it. That's why it's "living".
   - AR line: «اسمها الخطة الحية لأنها ما تنكتب مرة وتنتهي. تتعدل كل أسبوعين حسب ايش يصير مع طفلك فعلًا.»
   - Attracts: the mother who saw early progress on a program, then a setback, and felt she was back at zero.
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants a one-off consultation and a PDF. Not wrong, just not this.

7. **She decides, informed; she doesn't just follow instructions.** We explain the why, she knows her child, and the decisions are made together. Partnership, not expert-superiority.
   - AR line: «ما نعطيكي تعليمات تمشي عليها وبس. نشرح لك ليش، ونقرر الخطوة الجاية سوا.»
   - Attracts: the capable, thinking mother who wants to understand, not be managed.
   - Gently repels: the mother who wants to be told exactly what to do with no part in the thinking.

### The 2–3 lines the page must carry (non-negotiable)
1. «اللي تشوفيه كل يوم مو تفاصيل جانبية. هو أهم معلومة عن طفلك.»: early on the landing (near Anhar's intro / "how it works"), so she feels respected before she's sold to.
2. «ما نحاول نغيّر طفلك. نحاول نفهم يومه معاكي، ونختار أول شي نشتغل عليه.»: the belief behind the mechanism, right before or inside the "whole day, one area, every two weeks" section.
3. Who it's for / not for (near the price/CTA, calm, no judgment):
   «إذا تدوري علاج يشيل التوحد أو نتيجة مضمونة، هذي الخطة مو المكان المناسب لك. وإذا تدوري أحد يفهم يوم طفلك معاكي ويعدّل الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين، هذا اللي نسويه.»

Plus the locked core message (belief 5) wherever the brief already places it.

### Per-focus emphasis (which belief leads)
- A sleep: belief 1 + whole day ("bedtime is the end of his day").
- B outings/visits: belief 2 + 7 (she knows what helps him; we prepare the visit together). Respect for her, no labels even as quotes.
- C communication: belief 1 (the moments between speech sessions) + "alongside" his speech therapist.
- D food: belief 4 + 3 (no diet, safe foods stay, questions first).
- E transitions: belief 4 (not a five-minute warning for every child).
- F anger: belief 1 + 6 (whole day, and hard days are part of the plan); case 1 quote as one mother's experience.
- G / `?plan`: the belief statement + core message.
- Results card: belief 2 only, lightly. The result stays fully hers whether or not she joins.

## Emotional through-line

The feeling the page should leave: **"Someone finally looked at my whole day with me, and treated me as the one who knows him."**

- **Respect, not pity.** We speak to an intelligent woman who knows her child deeply. No "poor you", no sad imagery.
- **Partnership, not expert-superiority.** Anhar works *with* her ("أنا والقروب معاك"), never above her. No «أخيرًا بتفهمي».
- **Relief of clarity, not hype.** The relief is: one priority, a next step, someone who doesn't disappear between appointments. Calm, grounded tone; no exclamation-mark energy.
- **Belonging without pressure.** The first group of 10 mothers is mentioned once, as a fact, never as a push.
- **Hope without a promise.** We can promise the process (whole day, one area, review every two weeks). We never promise his outcome.

Arc: she's seen (her repeating moment) → she's respected (her observations matter) → she understands the approach (whole day, one area, every two weeks) → she's free to choose (who it's for/not for, monthly, cancel anytime).

---

## Lines we will NOT cross to avoid apathy

Strong conviction, never at someone's expense:
- **No blame.** Not of her ("you didn't try hard enough"), not of her family, not of other mothers' choices. «المشكلة مو إنك ما حاولتي كفاية» is the frame.
- **No shock.** No dramatic stats, no "before it's too late", no fear of regression, no countdowns.
- **No fake enemies.** Not against speech therapy, OT, centres, specialists or other parents. The only thing we stand against is *generic, one-size advice standing in for understanding*, and we say that calmly.
- **No promises.** No developmental or behavioural outcome (sleep, speech, eating, calm). Process yes, results no. Case quotes are labelled as one mother's experience.
- **No labels on the child.** Describe the observable scene, never عنيد/عدواني/مدلع/عصبي.
- **No clinical cause as fact.** We look at what happens before the moment; we don't diagnose on the page.
- **No pushing out with contempt.** The "not for you" line is respectful: her choice is valid, it's just a different path.
- **Not apathetic either.** Every section must carry one of the beliefs above. A line that any parenting brand could say ("خطة مخصصة لطفلك" alone, «ثقي بنفسك», «كل شي يبدأ بالحب») gets cut or tied to whole day / one area / every two weeks.
