## 0. Method and honesty notes (read first)

- ~25 web searches (Arabic + English). **Page fetching was blocked in this session** (aljazeera.net, youm7.com, hespress.com, khaleejtimes.com all failed DNS; reddit.com is refused by the search tool). So every "real quote" below came from the text of a search result snippet or a real page/thread title, **not read in full context**. Before any quote goes on the page as a quote, someone should open the URL and confirm it.
- **Saudi-specific verbatim mother speech is thin on the open web.** Saudi mothers talk in TikTok comments, Snapchat, WhatsApp groups and Instagram DMs, which search doesn't index. What I found: Saudi forum thread titles (عالم حواء), Gulf/Arab press stories, Saudi academic studies (themes only), and English parallels (Mumsnet, Reddit mirrors, UK autism forum).
- The best Saudi voice source we have is **the client's own material**: the owner's case quote ("كان يصحى معصب كل يوم. هالأسبوع صحى معصب مرة وحدة.") and the assessment's Q15 free text. Recommend the Copywriter treat Q15 answers + TikTok comments on Anhar's own posts as the primary voice-of-customer bank going forward.
- Legend: **[REAL]** = verbatim text as it appeared in source/snippet, with URL. **[TITLE]** = real thread/article title. **[PARAPHRASE]** = source's content in my words. **[SYNTHESIS]** = my inference / suggested Saudi phrasing. Never present SYNTHESIS as a mother's quote or testimonial.

## 1. Overall picture

### Pain points (the recurring scenes)
- Mothers carry the daily plan alone; specialists are seen in short sessions, home is where everything actually happens. The clearest statement of the brand's exact gap came from an English parent:
  - **[REAL]** "My daughter was getting PT/OT/Speech, but who was there with an everyday plan to put it together?" (r/Autism_Parenting, via mirror: https://redlib.belloworld.it/r/Autism_Parenting/comments/1b16g87/a_respectful_ponderance_about_the_antiaba)
- Isolation and loneliness, especially early on:
  - **[REAL]** "had to navigate everything completely on my own." / "It was a very lonely and desperate time." (UAE founder of a WhatsApp support group for mums of autistic children, Khaleej Times, Apr 2026: https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/support-group-help-mums-with-autistic-children)
- Panic and searching for help at the start:
  - **[TITLE]** "كيف اكتشف طفلي متوحد ولا لا ...بحاجة للمساعدة منهارة جدا" (عالم حواء, Saudi women's forum: https://hawaaworld.com/كيف_اكتشف_طفلي_متوحد_ولا_لا_بحاجة_للمساعدة_منهارة_جدا-4009666/page/2)
  - **[TITLE]** "مشكلتي مع ابني....." (عالم حواء: https://hawaaworld.com/مشكلتي_مع_ابني-71658)
  - **[TITLE]** "Lack of support for autism and don't know where to turn" (Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/Talk/special_needs/4216781-Lack-of-support-for-autism-and-don-t-know-where-to-turn-advice-appreciated)
  - **[TITLE]** "HELP PLEASE? Feel like I'm losing the war" (National Autistic Society forum: https://community.autism.org.uk/f/parents-and-carers/39566/help-please-feel-like-i-m-losing-the-war/386782)
- Stigma and shame in the Saudi context is documented academically: Jeddah study of 15 mothers lists themes "mothers' concerns… difficulty accessing resources; and cultural stigma (people's reactions)" **[PARAPHRASE of abstract]** (https://rdsjournal.org/index.php/journal/article/view/873; also https://sindex.sdl.edu.sa/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/EXPERIENCES-OF-MOTHERS-IN-DEALING-WITH/9936961808331). Medina study of family members: social exclusion and isolation; family/community support as "vital pillars" (https://drepo.sdl.edu.sa/items/30bce9ed-e81d-4feb-a218-4bc48f5d3f9b).

### What they've already tried (and why it didn't hold)
- Sessions (speech, OT, ABA, behaviour) without a home plan connecting them (quote above). Another parent: **[REAL]** "They've been doing hand over hand for 2 years and nothing is changing." (r/Autism_Parenting mirror: https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/Autism_Parenting/comments/1hvytrs/preschool_expectations_are_driving_me_crazy)
- Diets and "programs" passed around forums. On عالم حواء there are long threads for diets and branded home programs: **[TITLE]** "حمية غذائية كانت سببا بإذن الله في شفاء عدة أطفال مصابين بالتوحد" (https://hawaaworld.com/حمية_غذائية_كانت_سببا_بإذن_الله_في_شفاء_عدة_أطفال_مصابين_بالتوحد-4310866); **[TITLE]** "تجمع مطبقات برنامج اندلسيه دودي لعلاج التوحد" (163+ pages: https://hawaaworld.com/تجمع_مطبقات_برنامج_اندلسيه_دودي_لعلاج_التوحد-4585378/page/163). **[PARAPHRASE]** mothers report early improvement in sleep/food/hitting on a program, then e.g. the child "stays in bed for hours without sleeping"; another tried gluten/casein-free and saw no clear change. Pattern: try a promised fix → partial/temporary → back to searching.
- Ask-the-specialist threads where the answer is "hard to diagnose online" **[PARAPHRASE]** (https://hawaaworld.com/هذه_اسئلة_امهات_واجوبة_الاخصائي_مفيدة_لك-3602246). Generic answers frustrate.
- **[REAL]** "Speech therapy and ot haven't worked" (Mumsnet "what helped your autistic child": https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/am_i_being_unreasonable/4789054-to-ask-what-helped-your-autistic-child)

### Frustrations with existing solutions
- Sessions don't transfer to home; nobody stitches the pieces together (see quote above).
- Cost: Saudi press cites training/rehab for one autistic person at **"لا تقل عن 100 ألف ريال سنوياً"** **[REAL, snippet]** vs. government support of 20 ألف (Al-Watan, older/undated: https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/67442). Older Okaz report on Saudi families sending children to centres in Jordan due to service gaps (https://www.okaz.com.sa/article/1539241).
- Generic advice articles (webteb, mawdoo3, sayidaty) dominate Arabic search: lists of "tips", "causes", "signs". Mothers already have plenty of tips; this directly supports the brief's core line: انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر.
- Forum culture of "شفاء" claims creates both hope and burnout/scepticism.

### Beliefs
- Autism as something to be "cured" still appears (forum "شفاء" language). Brand must not echo it.
- Mother as the "key": **[REAL, headline]** "والأمّ مفتاح النجاح" (Hespress specialist interview: https://www.hespress.com/421878-421878.html). Double-edged: empowering, but easily becomes guilt. Use "she sees what no session sees", never "it's on you".
- Historic "refrigerator mother" blame is known in Arabic media (Al Jazeera: "الأم الثلاجة.. لماذا اتهمت الأمهات بتوريث التوحد لأطفالهن؟" https://www.aljazeera.net/women/2023/8/13/...) so mothers are sensitised to any hint of blame.
- Family/religious framing of patience (الصبر) is common in Arab coverage ("الصبر رفيق الأمهات", Masrawy title: https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_various/details/2016/4/5/781166/...).

### Objections to paying (mostly SYNTHESIS from the above, no direct quotes found)
- [SYNTHESIS] "جربت برامج قبل وما نفعت" (forum pattern of tried-programs).
- [SYNTHESIS] "عندنا جلسات أصلاً، ليش أدفع زيادة؟" (already paying centres; costs are high).
- [SYNTHESIS] "كيف تعرف ولدي وهي ما شافته؟" (online vs in-person; forum specialists say they can't judge online).
- [SYNTHESIS] "749 شهرياً كثير" vs. freelance session prices (see competitor section: $5–$20 sessions on Khamsat; 150 ريال home session ads).
- [SYNTHESIS] Fear of being judged / asked to do more when she's already exhausted.

### What they want their day to look like
- From the owner's case: the scene improves in countable terms ("صحى معصب مرة وحدة" instead of every day). Mothers measure in specific moments, not developmental milestones.
- [SYNTHESIS] To go to a family visit without dreading it; a bedtime that ends; to know what to do *next* tomorrow; someone who knows her child's history between appointments.

## 2. By focus area

### A. النوم (bedtime/sleep)
- **Scene:** resisting bed, stretching the pre-sleep routine, long time to fall asleep, night waking, very early waking. A Jeddah study of 40 parents found most common problems: sleep onset, then bedtime timing/consistency, then night waking **[PARAPHRASE]** (https://search.emarefa.net/detail/BIM-897174 / https://sero.journals.ekb.eg/article_91433.html).
- **Their words (English parallels):**
  - **[REAL]** "I'd hear him doing laps of his room at 3am" (https://medium.com/fit-yourself/autism-night-waking-sleep-anyone-a5ebaecb7671)
  - **[REAL]** "still isn't sleeping until 2/3am most nights" (Scope forum: https://forum.scope.org.uk/discussion/comment/752777)
  - **[REAL]** "without enough sleep, it can sometimes feel like torture" (theautismdad.com: https://theautismdad.com/2016/10/08/is-it-just-me-or-is-autism-parenting-synonymous-with-sleepless-nights)
  - **[TITLE]** "Sleep issues/screaming – at the end of my tether" (Mumsnet: https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/special_educational_needs/5398892-sleep-issuesscreaming-at-the-end-of-my-tether)
- Research: child sleep problems predict maternal stress "over and above symptom severity" **[PARAPHRASE]** (Haifa study: https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/sleep-problems-in-young-children-with-autism-spectrum-disorders-a/). Good framing: sleep is the mother's issue too.
- **Tried:** routines from articles, melatonin talk, diets/programs (forum).
- [SYNTHESIS, Saudi phrasing to test] "يسهر لين الفجر", "ينام وقت ويصحى ساعتين", "ما ينام إلا جنبي".
- **Market note:** Dubai Autism Center launched a behavioural sleep program (Jul 2026), "الأول من نوعه في المنطقة", based on home routine + environment changes (https://www.emaratalyoum.com/local-section/other/2026-07-05-1.2062014). Signals demand, and that "sleep" is a sellable entry point.

### B. الخروج والزيارات / العزايم
- **Scene:** declining dinner invitations, avoiding visits, going out rarely and only at quiet times, people's stares in restaurants/malls, relatives reading behaviour as bad parenting.
- **Their words:**
  - **[REAL, snippet]** "كنت أستمع باستمرار لمعاناة الأهالي وحرجهم من قبول دعوة عشاء عن امتناعهم عن كل أشكال الزيارات" (Al Jazeera blog "التوحد وأنا.. وخانتني العبرة": https://www.aljazeera.net/blogs/2023/1/15/التوحد-وأنا-وخانتني-العبرة)
  - **[REAL, snippet, Egyptian dialect]** "الناس بتقول على ابنى مش متربى" (mother quoted in Youm7: https://www.youm7.com/story/2016/4/3/آلام-نفسية-ومواقف-مُحرجة-كيف-يضاعف-المجتمع-متاعب-أسرة-الطفل/2657221)
  - **[REAL, snippet]** "كثيراً من الجالسين في ساحة المطعم الخارجية كانوا يتذمرون ويرمقون الطفل ووالديه بنظرات استياء" (Al Rai, writer observing a family: https://www.alraimedia.com/article/1763392)
  - **[REAL, snippet]** families hide the child: "إخفاء حقيقة وجود طفل مصاب بالتوحد في العائلة عن المحيط القريب" (webteb: https://www.webteb.com/articles/التوحد-autism-مرض-علاجه-لا-ينتهي_165)
  - **[TITLE]** "أنا أم لطفل مصاب بالتوحّد… ولم يحرق قلبي إلّا المتنمرون" (Raseef22: https://raseef22.net/article/1078129)
  - Omani mother **[PARAPHRASE]**: daughter has sudden anger episodes because she can't go out / do activities she loves (Oman Daily).
- **Tried:** staying home; going out only at weekends/off-peak; telling relatives in advance; short visits; quiet room at the host's (Omani author's Eid advice, https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1175848).
- **Saudi angle:** "العزايم" specifically didn't surface in search, but family gatherings (عيد، رمضان، بيت الجدة) are the culturally heavy moment. [SYNTHESIS] phrasings: "صرت أعتذر عن العزايم", "أخاف من نظراتهم", "يقولون دلعتيه".
- **Red flag:** do not repeat "مش متربى / مدلع" as anything but what *others* say; never let it read as the brand's view.

### C. التواصل
- **Scene:** child doesn't speak or speaks little; can't say what he wants; frustration → crying/hitting; mother guessing all day.
- **Their words:** Arabic search returned clinic articles, not mothers. [PARAPHRASE] forum Q&A: mothers asking whether non-speech means autism and when he'll talk (https://hawaaworld.com/كيف_اكتشف_طفلي_متوحد_ولا_لا_بحاجة_للمساعدة_منهارة_جدا-4009666/page/2).
- **Tried:** speech therapy (sometimes long-term, slow), screens/apps, waiting.
- Evidence note: "longer speech therapy helped about two-thirds develop spoken words" (2025 study via eremnews, https://www.eremnews.com/health/rlb54ww). Do NOT turn into a promise.
- Owner case 2 (communication plan, new behaviours after two weeks) is the only usable proof; keep exactly as supplied.
- [SYNTHESIS] "أحس إنه يبي يقول شي وأنا ما أفهمه", "يسحبني ليد الشي اللي يبيه".

### D. الأكل
- **Scene:** very short list of accepted foods; same type/colour/texture/brand; same plate/place/time; refusal of anything new; worry about weight and nutrition; family pressure at gatherings to "just make him eat".
- **Their words:** Hespress piece title **[TITLE]** "\"الانتقائية الغذائية\" .. وجبات الأكل تحبط عائلات \"أطفال التوحد\"" (https://www.hespress.com/536423-536423.html). Couldn't fetch the mothers' quotes inside.
- Prevalence: food selectivity in 84.8% in one Arab-region study **[PARAPHRASE]** (Journal of Taibah University Medical Sciences, 2021, Saudi: https://doaj.org/article/a77f7bd7dc214004bd68ca0d51755e91).
- English parallel: "safe foods", "only eats nuggets". (Reddit blocked; not quoted.)
- **Tried:** diets (GFCF), hiding foods, forcing, supplements; mixed/no results reported on forums.
- Owner case 3 fits exactly: extra questions first, gradual introduction while keeping safe foods. [SYNTHESIS] "ما ياكل إلا رز أبيض ونقتس", "أي أكل جديد يرجعه".

### E. الانتقال / إيقاف شي يحبه
- **Scene:** turning off the iPad/phone, leaving the park/mall, ending bath time, leaving the house in the morning; crying or screaming even after warnings.
- **Their words (English parallels):** [PARAPHRASE] parents report meltdowns at park exits "even with a five-minute warning" (podcast/blog results); screen-off cited as a top flashpoint (https://steadyneuro-site.pages.dev/guides/how-to-end-screen-time-without-a-meltdown). Mumsnet thread **[TITLE]** "Transitions/leaving fun places" (https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/parenting/5420211-transitionsleaving-fun-places).
- No Arabic mother verbatim found. [SYNTHESIS] "إذا سحبت منه الآيباد تقوم القيامة", "كل طلعة من الحديقة تنتهي بصياح".
- **Tried:** timers, warnings, bribes, giving in to avoid a scene. Why it fails (synthesis): applied inconsistently because nobody tracks *which* transitions are hard and why.

### F. الغضب والانفعال
- **Scene:** sudden anger, screaming, hitting others or himself, unkind words, long time to calm; mother can't predict when.
- **Their words:**
  - Owner's case: **[REAL, owner-supplied]** "كان يصحى معصب كل يوم. هالأسبوع صحى معصب مرة وحدة."
  - **[PARAPHRASE]** forum question: child with "عصبية وغضب كثير ونوم قليل، وأكل غير متنوع" (hawaaworld Q&A) — note how mothers bundle anger + sleep + food together. Strong support for "whole-day view".
  - **[TITLE]** "My Son Keeps Hitting Himself" (NAS forum: https://community.autism.org.uk/f/health-and-wellbeing/8519/my-son-keeps-hitting-himself); "6 year old hitting himself" (https://community.autism.org.uk/f/parents-and-carers/11933/6-year-old-hitting-himself)
  - **[REAL]** parent: ABA, psychiatry, OT and parenting therapy "clearly not working" (HealthUnlocked: https://healthunlocked.com/adhd-parents/posts/147499339/...)
- **Tried:** ignoring, punishing, giving in, medication (risperidone mentioned on forums), more sessions.
- **Safety flag:** self-injury content must always point to medical/specialist review; never imply the plan handles it alone.

## 3. Unsafe / off-brand to echo (for Copywriter + Test Panel)
- "شفاء" / "علاج التوحد" / "يتخلص من التوحد" (forum and diet language). Never.
- "مش متربى", "مدلع", "عنيد", "عدواني" — others' labels. Only ever as what people say *to* her, ideally not at all.
- "الأم مفتاح النجاح" → slides into blame. Prefer: she notices what no session sees.
- Causal claims from articles ("الحساسية الحسية هي السبب", melatonin, dopamine) — describe the scene, don't diagnose.
- Stats like 80% / 84.8% — fine for internal context, risky on page (source quality varies; brand has no stats of its own). Don't use.
- "الأول من نوعه" (Dubai Autism Center's claim) — don't mirror.
- Fear lines about the future (adults after 18 coverage) — no.

## 4. Competitor / alternative promises seen
| Alternative | What it offers / promises | Price signal | Gap vs الخطة الحية |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freelance specialists on Khamsat (online) | Behaviour-modification / speech sessions, "first session to collect case data and guide the parent"; "تقييم مبدئي وتوصيات عملية" | ~$5 (30-min psych consult), $10/hr, $20 per 45-min session (https://khamsat.com/lifestyle/life-coaching/3467311-..., /2553229-..., /4073759-...) | One-off, transactional; no continuity or review cycle |

| Private centres / rehab (Saudi) | Full-time training/rehab | "لا تقل عن 100 ألف ريال سنوياً" (older Al-Watan figure); historic Jordan centres $500–700/mo (2013) | Expensive; skill stays in the centre |
| Dubai Autism Center sleep program (2026) | Behavioural sleep program, home routine + environment, avoids medication; "الأول من نوعه في المنطقة" | Not published | Single problem, UAE-based, clinic-led |
| Saudi charities: جمعية أسر التوحد (≈7,500 beneficiaries, 5 branches), معك (Medina), Tabuk association | Family guidance/counselling, awareness, short parent training (2019 study showed improved parental expectations) | Free/low-cost; access varies | Not personalised, not continuous, waitlists/geography |
| WHO Caregiver Skills Training (online, with Autism Speaks) | Free skills training delivered by non-specialists | Free | Generic, not Arabic-Saudi-personalised |
| Forum "programs"/diets (برنامج أندلسية, GFCF, pumpkin recipes) | Implicit/explicit "شفاء" | Free / product cost | Overpromise; mothers burn out → scepticism to fight |
| Generic Arabic content (webteb, mawdoo3, sayidaty, Instagram tips) | "10 نصائح" lists | Free | Exactly the "more tips" the brand positions against |

**Positioning read:** the market splits into (a) cheap one-off sessions, (b) expensive centre-based therapy, (c) free generic advice / forum cures. Nobody visible in search offers an ongoing, personalised, mother-facing home plan with a fixed review rhythm, in Saudi dialect. 749/month sits above one-off sessions but far below centre costs; the justification is continuity + personalisation + someone "with her" between appointments, not more sessions. No Arabic parent-coaching subscription with public monthly pricing was found (searched; may exist on Instagram/TikTok unindexed).
