# Positioning: مدار البيت | الخطة الحية (Specialist 4)

Built on: brief.md, research.md, market.md (level 3, mechanism market, with a Stage-5 emotional heart) and audit.md (58/100).
Client confirmation applied: the first group is genuinely **10 mothers**, **monthly, cancel anytime**. Market.md's "invented ١٠ أمهات" warning no longer applies. The line is true and may be used **once**, plainly, with no countdown.

---

## 1. Core positioning

**EN:** The Living Plan is personal, ongoing guidance from Anhar for Saudi mothers of autistic children who already have tips, sessions and programs, but nobody helping them turn their child's real day into one plan. We look at his whole day, work on one area at a time, and adjust the plan with her every two weeks, alongside his specialists, not instead of them.

**AR:** الخطة الحية متابعة شخصية مع أنهار، لكل أم جربت نصائح وجلسات وبرامج وما زالت نفس المواقف تتكرر. نبدأ من يوم طفلك كامل، نشتغل على مجال واحد، ونعدّل الخطة معاكي كل أسبوعين، مع جلساته مو بدالها.

(Confirm the Arabic spelling of the owner's name, أنهار, before it ships.)

Against the alternatives she has tried:
- **Tips and articles** give her more information. We give her **one priority and the next step**.
- **Sessions and centres** work with the child for an hour. We work with **her, on the other 23 hours**, and connect home life to what the specialists do.
- **Programs and diets** are fixed and promise a result. Our plan **changes with her child**, and we promise no result.

Who it is for: mothers who believe growth happens at home, who want support and not judgment, and who don't see autism as something to fix. It is not for someone looking for a cure or a guaranteed result. The page should let that mother leave.

## 2. Unique mechanism

**Decision:** we do **not** coin a new term. The mechanism is three plain steps, and the product's own name explains it: the plan is "living" because it is adjusted every two weeks. That is the only "naming" we do. It is honest and nobody else can claim it without doing the work.

**EN, the mechanism in one line:**
**Whole day, one area, every two weeks.** We look at the child's whole day, not just the moment that keeps repeating. We choose one area to work on. Every two weeks we review it with her and adjust it to how he responded, including hard days, setbacks and changes in routine.

**AR, the mechanism in one line:**
**يومه كامل، مجال واحد، مراجعة كل أسبوعين.**
نشوف يوم طفلك كامل، مو الموقف وحده. نختار مجال واحد نشتغل عليه. وكل أسبوعين نراجعه معاكي ونعدّله حسب استجابته، حتى لو مرت أيام صعبة أو تغيّر الروتين.

**Why it's called "living" (AR, for the page):**
اسمها الخطة الحية لأنها ما تنكتب مرة وتنتهي. تتعدل كل أسبوعين حسب ايش يصير مع طفلك فعلًا.

**Proof the mechanism is real (use exactly as supplied, no added details):**
- Whole day: case 1. Instead of focusing on the anger alone, the questions were about his whole day, and sensitivity and pressure turned out to be part of the picture. Owner quote: «كان يصحى معصب كل يوم. هالأسبوع صحى معصب مرة وحدة.» Label it as one mother's experience, not a typical result.
- Questions first, then one area: case 3 (food). Extra questions first so other needs aren't missed, then new foods introduced gradually while his safe foods stay.
- One area: case 2 (communication). A communication plan; after two weeks, new behaviours started to appear. Don't make it more specific than that.

## 3. Unique value proposition

**EN:** She gets someone who knows her child's history and stays with her between appointments: a plan built from his day and from what she has already tried, one clear priority at a time, and a fixed review every two weeks so she never starts from zero. Generic advice can't do this because it doesn't know her child. Clinic-only support can't do it because it doesn't see the home.

**AR:** معاكي أحد يعرف قصة طفلك وما يختفي بين المواعيد. خطة مبنية من يومه ومن اللي جربتيه قبل، أولوية وحدة واضحة، ومراجعة ثابتة كل أسبوعين عشان ما تبدئي من الصفر كل مرة.

What is included (from the brief, nothing added): an extended intake questionnaire; a personal plan focused on one main area at a time; an interactive review every two weeks; ongoing adjustments; tracking to see patterns over time; a whole-life view (sleep, siblings, routine, environment); «أنا والقروب معاك» between reviews. Bonuses: خريطة نقطة البداية and مجتمع واتساب. ٧٤٩ ريال / الشهر, monthly, cancel anytime.

**AR (locked, from the brief):**
**انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي طريقة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم.**

«انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي خطة تبدأ من يوم طفلك، وتتعدل معاكي كل أسبوعين.»

## 5. Differentiators

1. **We start from his whole day, not the behaviour alone.** Bedtime is the end of his day, and anger has things that build up before it. Case 1 is the proof.
2. **One area at a time, not ten tips a week.** She leaves each review knowing the one priority and the next step.
3. **Reviewed and adjusted every two weeks.** The plan changes with how he responds, including hard days, setbacks and routine changes. It is not a one-off session or a fixed program.
4. **A real person and a small group, in her dialect.** Anhar, plus the group of mothers between reviews. The first group takes 10 mothers. That is true, so say it once.
5. **It works alongside his specialists, with low risk to her.** It connects home life to his therapy and doesn't replace it. Monthly, cancel anytime, and no promised outcome.

## 6. Messaging angles

### Use
- **Her repeating moment first, then the how.** The assessment already asked «ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك؟», so the landing answers it: the scene, then "we look at the whole day".
- **"You see what no session sees."** Her observations are real information. This replaces «الأم مفتاح النجاح».
- **"You've tried enough. The problem isn't effort."** Keep the live reframe: «المشكلة مو إنك ما حاولتي كفاية…» (fix the typos).
- **Continuity:** someone who already knows her child between appointments, so she isn't starting over.
- **Honest risk reducers, said plainly:** monthly, cancel anytime; not a replacement for therapy; no guaranteed result; what happens after the Google Form (we contact her; her place isn't confirmed until then).
- **Honest scarcity, once:** «الدفعة الأولى تستقبل ١٠ أمهات فقط.» One mention, with no countdown or deadline.
- **Who:** introduce Anhar early, so «أنا» means someone.

### Avoid
- Outcome promises: «ينام أسرع»، «يهدأ»، «يبدأ يتكلم»، «ياكل أكثر».
- Cure language: «علاج»، «شفاء»، «يتخلص من».
- "More content" words: «نصائح»، «أسرار»، «دليل شامل» (except when saying she doesn't need more of them).
- Superlatives and firsts: «الأول من نوعه»، «أفضل برنامج».
- Generic coach phrases on their own: «خطة مخصصة لطفلك»، «طريق واضح تمشي عليه». Always tie them to whole day, one area, or every two weeks.
- Blame or pressure: «الأم مفتاح النجاح»، «لازم تلتزمي».
- Labels on the child: «عنيد»، «عدواني»، «مدلع»، «عصبي». Other people's judgment appears only as something said to her, and rarely.
- Fear, guilt, "before it's too late", countdowns, or a second mention of the 10 places.
- Anything against speech therapy, OT or other specialists.
- Clinical causes stated as fact (sensory, dopamine, anxiety). Describe the scene.

## 7. How positioning flexes per situation

Same mechanism every time. Only the part we stress changes.

- **A, sleep:** bedtime is the end of his whole day, so we look at the day, not just the bed. One adjustment at a time, reviewed every two weeks. Her tiredness can be named. No promise about hours of sleep.
- **B, outings and visits:** lead with her (the invitation, بيت الجدة, the looks), then a light mechanism: prepare the visit from what we know about his day and what helps him. Short headlines. No labels, even as quotes.
- **C, communication:** the moments at home between speech sessions, built around what he's trying to say during his day. Complementary to the speech therapist, never against. No promise of words.
- **D, food:** the opposite of a diet. Questions first, his safe foods stay, new foods come gradually (case 3). No promise about the food list or weight.
- **E, transitions:** which transitions are hard for him and what comes before them, instead of a five-minute warning for every child. Use a concrete scene (the iPad, leaving the park).
- **F, anger and outbursts:** the strongest proof. Lead with whole day, not the outburst alone, with case 1's quote close by as one mother's experience. If he hits himself, point to a doctor or specialist, and never suggest the plan handles that alone.
- **G, other / `?plan` / missing:** lead with the core message plus the full one-line mechanism. For G, reflect her own words (free text or Q15) in the subheadline instead of guessing a scene.
