## Recommendation: d1, مشهدها أولًا، ثم الطريقة

d1 is the only direction that does both jobs market.md sets for the headline: she recognises her own repeating moment first (the identification beat that answers «ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر؟», which she just took), then gets one mechanism element. It also personalizes most natively: each Q14 situation has its own scene in her words, and the lowest dimension swaps only the second half (see dimension variants), so the matrix never collapses into one sentence with a word swapped. d2 is sharper on F but turns abstract on B and D; d3 carries the belief beautifully but its core lines are better placed under the headline and near Anhar's intro, where they won't repeat.
