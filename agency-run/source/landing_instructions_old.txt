PART 1 — BUILD INSTRUCTIONS
These instructions are for the AI website builder. They are not visible to visitors.
1. Page purpose and conversion goal
Build a personalized, mobile-first landing page for مدار البيت (Home Orbit).
This page appears immediately after a mother completes the assessment:
"ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك؟"
The mother has already received her assessment results. The purpose of this page is to connect those results to the value of الخطة الحية (The Living Plan) and encourage her to join the waitlist.
The primary conversion is:
Mother clicks the waitlist CTA → she is redirected to an external Google Form.
There is no success message, confirmation screen, or form submission on this landing page itself. Do not create an on-page waitlist form.
The Google Form URL will be provided separately. Store it as a configurable destination and use it for every waitlist CTA.
2. Required assessment data
Use the actual results from the completed assessment. Do not ask the mother to take the assessment again.
The landing page should have access to these three values:
A. Overall profile
One of the following:
* البداية تحتاج وضوح
* عندك أساس، والخطوة الجاية تنظيمه
* عندك طريقة واضحة، وحان وقت تطويرها
B. Lowest-scoring dimension
One of the following:
* فهم الأنماط المتكررة
* تخصيص الطريقة لطفلك
* وضوح الخطة واستمراريتها
C. Answer to Question 14
The specific situation the mother selected as the one she most wants to improve. Possible examples include:
* وقت النوم
* الزيارات والعزايم
* التواصل والتعبير عن الاحتياجات
* الأكل
* الانتقال من نشاط إلى آخر
* الانفعالات والمواقف الصعبة
Use the actual answer options and stored answer values from the assessment builder. Do not invent new values or assume that every possible situation appears in Question 14.
If a value is missing, use the relevant fallback copy described below. Never display a variable name, a blank value, a technical error, or a guessed result to the mother.
3. Headline personalization logic
The headline must be generated from Question 14 and the lowest-scoring dimension. The overall profile is supporting context, not the primary headline.
The mother should feel that the page is speaking to her specific situation—not that the builder has inserted her assessment result into a generic sales message.
Apply the following logic in order:
Step 1: Identify the situation from Question 14
This determines the real-life scene or challenge the headline refers to.
For example:
* Bedtime → a recurring bedtime struggle.
* Family visits → the difficulty that repeats during gatherings.
* Communication → difficulty expressing needs or making requests.
* Eating → mealtimes, food refusal, or a narrow range of accepted foods.
* Transitions → difficulty moving between activities.
* Emotional regulation → recurring difficult moments when the child becomes upset.
Use language that accurately represents the answer she selected. Do not invent details about her child or exaggerate the severity of the situation.
Step 2: Select the headline angle from the lowest-scoring dimension
If the lowest-scoring dimension is فهم الأنماط المتكررة:
Focus on noticing what happens repeatedly and understanding the circumstances surrounding the situation.
Example for bedtime:
"كل ليلة وقت النوم نفس الموقف؟ ابدئي بملاحظة ايش يتكرر قبله."
If the lowest-scoring dimension is تخصيص الطريقة لطفلك:
Focus on moving beyond general advice and choosing an approach that fits her child.
Example for bedtime:
"وقت النوم ما له حل واحد يناسب كل الأطفال. طفلك يحتاج طريقة تناسبه."
If the lowest-scoring dimension is وضوح الخطة واستمراريتها:
Focus on having a clear next step instead of repeatedly changing approaches or starting over.
Example for bedtime:
"كل ليلة تجربي حل جديد؟ خلّي عندك خطة واضحة لوقت النوم."
These are examples of the personalization logic. Generate natural, grammatically correct Saudi Arabic for the mother's actual selected situation.
Step 3: Use the overall profile to personalize the supporting sentence
The supporting sentence should reflect the mother's current stage without repeating her profile label mechanically.
For البداية تحتاج وضوح:
Emphasize finding a manageable starting point and choosing one priority.
Example:
"ابدئي بالموقف اللي يشغلك أكثر، وحددي خطوة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم اليومية."
For عندك أساس، والخطوة الجاية تنظيمه:
Acknowledge that she has already tried approaches or gathered observations, and explain how a structured plan can organize those efforts.
Example:
"رتّبي ملاحظاتك ومحاولاتك في خطة واضحة، بخطوات عملية تركّزي عليها خلال الأسبوعين الجايين."
For عندك طريقة واضحة، وحان وقت تطويرها:
Respect her existing efforts and emphasize refinement rather than starting over.
Example:
"ابني على اللي تعرفيه عن طفلك، وطوّري خطتك بناء على استجابته والمواقف اللي تعيشوها."
Do not imply that the profile is a diagnosis, a judgment of parenting ability, or a fixed description of the mother.
Step 4: Combine the values
The headline should combine:
Specific situation + insight from the lowest-scoring dimension.
The supporting sentence should combine:
Personalized next step + context appropriate to the overall profile.
Do not put all three assessment values into the headline. That will make it long and mechanical.
Do not show the mother her score again unless there is a compelling design reason. She has already seen her results.
Step 5: Fallback behavior
If Question 14 is unavailable, use a general headline appropriate to the lowest-scoring dimension.
If the lowest-scoring dimension is unavailable, use the specific situation from Question 14 and a general supporting message about a personalized plan.
If both values are unavailable, use:
Headline:
"بدل ما تجربي نصايح متفرقة، ابدئي بخطة تناسب طفلك."
Supporting sentence:
"الخطة الحية تساعدك تركّزي على تحدٍّ واحد، وتطبقي خطوات عملية في حياتكم اليومية، وتراجعي استجابة طفلك كل أسبوعين."
If the overall profile is unavailable, use a neutral supporting sentence. Do not block the page from loading because one value is missing.
4. Page structure and section order
Build the page in this exact order:
1. Personalized headline and supporting sentence.
2. The recurring-problem section.
3. Introduction to الخطة الحية.
4. How the service works.
5. Benefits of an organized, personalized approach.
6. Offer card, including the monthly price and included support.
7. Success stories and case studies.
8. Bonuses.
9. Objection-handling cards.
10. Final waitlist CTA.
11. Frequently asked questions in expandable dropdowns.
The page should be concise and easy to scan. The audience includes busy mothers who may be reading on their phones while managing their children and household responsibilities.
Use short paragraphs, clear headings, sufficient spacing, and expandable sections where appropriate. Avoid long uninterrupted text walls.
Do not make the page primarily about the company. Keep the focus on the mother's situation, what she needs, how the service works, and what she receives.
5. Conversion principles
Apply these principles naturally:
Clarity: Within the first screen, the mother should understand that the page relates to the situation she just assessed and that a personalized plan is the next step.
Credibility: Use the real case studies supplied below. Present them accurately. Do not invent testimonials, credentials, statistics, client counts, or guaranteed outcomes.
Specificity: Use real-life examples and explain what the service actually includes, especially the review and plan adjustments every two weeks.
Urgency: Do not create fake countdowns, invented deadlines, or unsupported claims that places are about to disappear. The page can communicate the value of addressing a recurring challenge without pressuring the mother.
Emotional connection: Acknowledge the difficulty of repeated situations without blaming the mother or presenting her child as a problem to fix.
Strong, confident wording: Avoid unnecessary uncertainty words such as "يمكن" and "ممكن" when describing what the service includes. Be confident about the process, but never guarantee a particular developmental or behavioral outcome.
6. CTA and waitlist behavior
Use the following primary CTA label consistently:
"احجزي مكانك في قائمة الانتظار"
This communicates a concrete action and makes clear that the mother is joining a waitlist rather than purchasing immediately on the page.
Place the CTA:
* Once after the offer card.
* Once near the end of the page.
The CTA must open the external Google Form provided by the owner.
Use a clear button with a strong visual contrast. Do not use vague labels such as "اضغطي هنا" or "اعرفي المزيد."
Do not:
* Create a payment flow.
* Collect the mother's information on this landing page.
* Display a fabricated success message.
* Claim that her place is confirmed.
* Invent a Google Form URL.
If the destination URL has not been configured, make the missing configuration obvious to the site owner rather than sending visitors to a broken or invented link.
7. Success-story integrity
The case studies below are based on the owner's descriptions of previous experiences.
Preserve their actual meaning and reported outcomes. Do not claim that the service treats, diagnoses, or cures any condition. Do not present a reported improvement as proof that the plan alone caused the change.
Do not invent names, photos, dates, credentials, or additional details.
Use the stories to illustrate the process: asking broader questions, understanding the context, creating a personalized plan, and reviewing what happened.
8. FAQ behavior
Render each FAQ as an accessible accordion/dropdown.
Show the question by default and reveal the answer when the visitor expands it. Make the controls keyboard-accessible and usable on mobile.
The FAQ must not replace important offer information. The price, core service, ongoing guidance, and waitlist CTA should remain visible in the main page.
