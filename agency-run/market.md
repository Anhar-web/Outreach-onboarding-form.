# Market level: 3 (Mechanism), with a Stage-5 heart

**In one line:** The headline must lead with **her own repeating moment, and then the *how***: one plan built from her child's *whole day*, focused on one area, and adjusted every two weeks to how he responds. It must not lead with a result.

---

## Why: evidence from research.md

**1. The claim "we'll help your child with X" is worn out. Mothers hear it all the time and many have been let down by it.** By the book, nobody in Arabic sells this exact offer: research §4 found no ongoing, personalized home plan for mothers, in Saudi dialect, with a fixed review rhythm. But the *benefit* claim (calmer child, better sleep, eating, talking) has been made to them many times, from three directions:
- **Cure and program promises:** «حمية غذائية كانت سببا بإذن الله في شفاء عدة أطفال مصابين بالتوحد» [TITLE], and «تجمع مطبقات برنامج اندلسيه دودي لعلاج التوحد», which runs to 163+ pages [TITLE]. Mothers report early gains that don't last, or "no clear change" [PARAPHRASE]. The pattern is: try a promised fix, see a partial or temporary change, then go back to searching.
- **Sessions that don't carry over to home:** "who was there with an everyday plan to put it together?" [REAL, EN]; "They've been doing hand over hand for 2 years and nothing is changing." [REAL, EN]; "Speech therapy and ot haven't worked" [REAL, EN]; ABA, psychiatry and OT "clearly not working" [REAL, EN].
- **Generic advice everywhere:** webteb, mawdoo3, sayidaty and Instagram all run "١٠ نصائح" lists. That is exactly the brief's line: «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر».

When a market has heard the benefit this often and stopped believing it, it is at Stage 3. A bigger or more specific promise (Stage 2) would sound like one more «شفاء» thread.

**2. It is not Stage 4.** Mothers have not yet heard many competing *mechanisms* for a home plan. The alternatives sell a format (a session, a centre, a diet, a tips list), not a process. "We look at your child's whole day, work on one area at a time, and review every two weeks" is **unclaimed**. A plain, credible mechanism is enough. It does not need to be "better than other mechanisms".

**3. The split: the market is at Stage 3, but the mothers are emotionally at Stage 5.** Their lived state is exhaustion, isolation and sensitivity to blame:
- "had to navigate everything completely on my own" / "a very lonely and desperate time" [REAL, UAE]
- «…بحاجة للمساعدة منهارة جدا» [TITLE, Saudi forum]
- Relatives' judgement: «الناس بتقول على ابنى مش متربى» [REAL, Egyptian]
- The historic "refrigerator mother" blame (Al Jazeera). Mothers notice any hint of blame.

What this means for the copy: **open with identification** (her scene, in her words, without blame), then **move straight to the mechanism**. Don't open with a product pitch, and don't open with a promise. The best proof that this order works is the owner's own case, which has both halves: the scene «كان يصحى معصب كل يوم…» and the how «بدل ما نركز على التصرف وحده، سألتها عن يومه كامل».

**4. The open lane, in one sentence.** Everyone else offers a *session*, a *program* or a *tip*. Nobody offers **a person who looks at the child's whole day with the mother and keeps adjusting one plan with her every two weeks**, in her dialect, between and alongside the specialists. That is our lane. The audit (item 4) confirms that the live page describes the mechanism generically and never names the "whole day" differentiator.

## The headline must lead with
1. **Her specific repeating moment** (from Q14), in Saudi words. This is the identification beat. It answers the assessment title «ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك؟».
2. **The *how*, pointed at that moment.** The lowest dimension picks which part of the mechanism to stress:
   - **Dim 0 (فهم الأنماط):** *seeing the pattern.* We look at his whole day to see what builds up to this moment, not only the moment itself.
   - **Dim 1 (تخصيص الطريقة):** *built from your child.* The plan is built from his day, his sensitivities and what you've already tried, not a rule for every child.
   - **Dim 2 (وضوح الخطة واستمراريتها):** *the rhythm.* One area at a time, reviewed and adjusted every two weeks, so she isn't starting over each time. **Each dim-2 cell needs its own wording.** The audit found all six reduce to «طريق واضح».
3. The headline names her moment **plus one mechanism element**, never all three, and never the profile. The subheadline carries the profile ("start with clarity", "organize what you have", "develop what works") and says what this is: personal guidance from Anhar, reviewed every two weeks, paid monthly.

The working name of the mechanism, for the Positioning Strategist to finalize, is: **«نشوف يومه كامل، مو الموقف وحده»** (whole day, not the behavior alone), plus **«مجال واحد في كل مرة»** (one area at a time), plus **«مراجعة كل أسبوعين»** (review every two weeks). Keep it in plain words. Don't coin a branded or technical term (the Dubai centre's «الأول من نوعه» style).

## Avoid leading with
- **Outcome promises:** «ينام أسرع», «يهدأ», «يبدأ يتكلم», «ياكل أكثر». They are banned by the brief, and this market has heard them from the «شفاء» threads.
- **"Cure / treat" language:** «علاج», «شفاء», «يتخلص من».
- **"More tips/info":** «نصائح», «أسرار», «دليل شامل». This is exactly what she's tired of.
- **Superlatives and firsts:** «الأول من نوعه», «أفضل برنامج». Stage-2 moves sound like every program thread.
- **Formula phrases** that any coach could say: «خطة مخصصة لطفلك» on its own, or «طريق واضح تمشي عليه». They aren't wrong, but they are generic unless tied to the whole-day mechanism.
- **Blame or "you're the key" framing:** «الأم مفتاح النجاح», «لازم تلتزمي». Use "you see what no session sees" instead.
- **Labels on the child:** «عنيد», «عدواني», «مدلع». Others' judgement can appear only as what people say *to* her, and rarely.
- **Fear and urgency:** the future, "before it's too late", countdowns, and the invented «١٠ أمهات فقط».

## Arabic hook directions (for the Copywriter; directions, not final lines)

All of these follow the same order: scene, then *how*, with no promised result. Use ايش (not وش), feminine verbs without the extra ن, and no blame.

1. **Pattern angle (dim 0), anger:**
   «نفس الغضب يتكرر كل يوم؟ نبدأ من يومه كامل، مو من لحظة الانفجار بس.»
2. **Customization angle (dim 1), food:**
   «خطة أكل تبدأ من الأكل اللي يرتاح له… ومن أسئلة عن يومه قبل أي تجربة جديدة.»
3. **Rhythm angle (dim 2), sleep:**
   «وقت النوم ما يتغير بنصيحة وحدة. مجال واحد، خطوة وحدة، ونراجعها مع بعض كل أسبوعين.»
4. **Identity angle (Stage-5 opener), outings:**
   «صرتي تحسبي ألف حساب قبل أي عزيمة؟ نجهز لها من اللي نعرفه عن يومه وايش يريحه.»
5. **Core and fallback (?plan):**
   «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي خطة تمشي مع يوم طفلك… وتتعدل معك كل أسبوعين.»

Phrases from research.md marked [SYNTHESIS], such as «يسهر لين الفجر» or «إذا سحبت منه الآيباد تقوم القيامة», may be used as *our* descriptive wording to test. **They must never be presented as a mother's quote or testimonial.** The only real Saudi mother quote available is the owner's case 1 line.
