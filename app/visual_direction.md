# Visual Direction: Batch 1 (10 static carousels)

## 1. What's working now

1. **Slide 1 does the work.** The first slide decides the swipe. Use one bold, high-contrast line plus a visible promise: the question big, the second clause small. ([trymypost](https://www.trymypost.com/blog/instagram-carousel-hook-design-principles-2026), [postnitro](https://postnitro.ai/blog/post/instagram-carousel-design))
2. **One focal point that reads as a thumbnail.** One object or event, nothing competing. ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-photography-tips), [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/scroll-stopping-ad-images))
3. **Handmade beats polished.** 2026 forecasts point to paper texture and collage as a reaction against AI-smooth visuals. ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/graphic-design/texture-warmth-and-tactile-rebellion-the-big-graphic-design-trends-for-2026), [Inkish](https://inkish.news/en/article/2026-design-trend-forecast-says-brands-will-win-with-imperfect-human-visuals))
4. **One metaphor, layered paper, real shadow.** Eiko Ojala is the benchmark: minimal, lit, one idea per piece. ([Colossal](https://www.thisiscolossal.com/2017/10/new-paper-textured-editorial-illustrations-by-eiko-ojala/))
5. **Step carousels with visible progress.** Numbering, one idea and ≤4 lines per slide, and a final slide that looks different. ([kivopost](https://kivopost.com/glossary/content-types-creative-formats-styles/step-by-step-carousel))
6. **Colour-blocking in a checkerboard.** Flat colour fields are legible. Rotating 2–3 grounds across posts keeps the grid from blurring. ([colorpick](https://colorpick-app.pages.dev/blog/color-social-media-design))
7. **Photo + paper hybrids.** Real photos with cut-paper overlays, a growing editorial pattern. ([visie](https://visie.io/article/the-art-of-the-mash-up-7-collage-illustrators-turning-chaos-into-beauty))

**Arabic:**
- Design RTL-native; don't mirror a Latin layout.
- Numerals don't mirror.
- Heavy weights get dark, so cap El Messiri at 700.
- Swipe order runs right to left. ([type guide](https://englishtoarabic-org.pages.dev/calligraphy/typography/))

**Caveat:** The performance numbers come from vendor blogs, so treat them as directional. That includes TikTok carousels "+81% engagement vs video" ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/)) and [Socialinsider's](https://www.socialinsider.io/data-geeks/Instagram-Carousel-Study.pdf) carousel ER. I found no study comparing text-led and illustrated carousels, so §5 tests it.

## 2. Recent Home Orbit work

- Paper puppets for the mother and child.
- Scattered props: checklists, a clock, toys.
- Text on a teal strip rotated −1° at the top.

**"Leaving Grandma's":**
- The same side-view room diorama, warm and in daylight: cream, brown furniture, sofa, door, three figures.

**Repetition risk:**
- The same wide room, the same puppets and the same top text label. Ten more dioramas would make the grid identical.
- The paper also reads as vector rather than paper.

## 3. The look for this batch

**Through-line (recognisable):**
- **Real paper material.** Visible grain, edges that are slightly irregular rather than vector-perfect, and a tight contact shadow plus a soft cast shadow. Shapes overlap.
- **Type.** El Messiri 700 for hooks at 88–110px. Tajawal 500 for body at 46–54px. Max 3 lines per slide.
- **Signature step tab.** Labels («أول شي», «الخطوة الثانية», «الحساب الثالث») sit on a small torn sand-paper tab, top-right, with paper progress dots.
- **Endings.** Every conversion post ends on a sand ground with the same paper "link in bio" tag. Attention posts end on one quiet line.
- **No faces on the child.** Show hands, his back, feet and objects. This is respectful, avoids puppets, and is easier to render.

**What varies:** composition family, ground colour (checkerboard across the grid), point of view (side, top-down, macro), and hero scale.

**Grounds:** sand #C9935A, light teal #DCE8EA, deep teal #005A70, paper white #F7F2EA, and deep brown #3A2410 (once).

## 6. Production realism (HTML/CSS → PNG)

**Renders well in Chromium at 1080×1350:**
- `clip-path` and SVG cut shapes.
- Grain via SVG `feTurbulence` (fractalNoise at about 0.04 and 4–5 octaves, 6–10% opacity). See [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2019/02/19/svg-filter-effects-creating-texture-with-feturbulence/) and [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/?p=351302).
- Torn edges via `feDisplacementMap` or jagged polygons.
- Layered shadows.
- Arabic RTL with El Messiri and Tajawal.
- Tabs and dots, maps, clocks and flat food shapes.
- Type-only slides.
- Panoramas: render one wide canvas, then slice it.

**Needs Canva, illustration or photos:**
- Large figurative hands and bodies (keep them small and simplified, or use SVG illustration).
- Realistic food.
- The p1/p4 photo bases: source in Canva or stock, then composite paper in HTML.
- A one-time real paper scan as an uploaded texture would beat procedural grain.

**Rule:** 6–12 shapes per slide. Detail costs legibility.
