### Patterns to learn (apply to every future hook)
1. **One question, no second half.** She dropped every second half. The first line carries it.
2. **«ما تقدري … إلا …» is her shape** for the workaround: three of her twelve use it. It sits beside the winners' «فصرتي…».
3. **Past tense for one real occurrence** (جلستي، قمتي، قلتي له، وديتيه), present tense for the routine.
4. **Check physics and real habits.** Can she hear it? Do mothers actually do this? A false detail kills the hook.
5. **There must be a real problem at stake.** If it can wait, it's a statement, not a hook.
6. **End on the problem she has now** (ما يرضى ينام لحاله), not on clever irony.
7. **Spellings to keep as written:** تنوميه، اثنين، ثمانيه، عشره، الاستشوار، صالون الاطفال، قُمتي، جنبك / جمبه (both).
