## Gate (Arabic legibility)

All 10 posts pass the Arabic legibility gate: verify.py verbatim PASS on all 10; El Messiri/Tajawal only; RTL; all text >=44px; no broken ligatures, clipping or text behind art seen in full-size review; contrast >=4.5:1 everywhere (lowest ink on gold 5.3:1). ## Batch verdict

- **Dimensions:** All 81 slide PNGs are 1080x1350 (4:5). Correct for Instagram.
- **Blue leading:** Yes. 6 of 10 covers are deep/night-led (p5, p2, p3, p4, p1, plus p7's deep left panel), and deep is the main motif colour on p9 and p8. Pearl is a ground only on p8, as the override requires. Watch-outs: p6 is fully gold, and p1's lawn puts a large field of bright teal on the grid.
- **Grid:** Recognisable, not identical. The paper grain, torn edges, gold tabs and El Messiri hooks hold the feed together, while composition changes every post (macro, split, plan, board, panorama, radial, flat-lay, scale, map, type). In a 3-column profile the light and dark covers make a near checkerboard. This is a clear step away from the older wide side-view room scenes.
- **Too similar:**
  - p7 and p4: both use a round gold plate of dotted round biscuits/nuggets on a deep ground. Change one plate/food treatment.
  - p3 is the one post still in the old side-view room genre (bed, window, lamp, floor line). It is the most at risk of 'more of the same'.
- **Fix first:**
  - p2 cover: zoom out so the majlis reads at a glance (lowest attention score).
  - p4/p7 plate duplication.
  - p9 cover crop so the fridge reads as a fridge.
