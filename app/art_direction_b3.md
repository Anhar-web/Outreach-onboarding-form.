# Batch 3 art direction (Visual Researcher + Art Director, condensed)

## What the data says (Analyst, B1–B2)
- **Light grounds: 0 of 10 broke out. Dark (deep/night): 6 of 12.** Batch 3 rule: **slide 1 is always deep or night**, and the whole post stays on its dark ground. Light colours appear only as accents (pearl, gold, teal paper) and on the **gold CTA end**.
- Breakouts averaged 3.0–3.5 slides viewed, so slides 2–3 must earn the swipe. The motif **evolves** (state, scale, position), so the swipe reads as a story.

## Through-line (hold it on every post): unchanged from B2
Cut paper (kit `paper()` / `torn_*`), grain, light from the top-right. Deep `#005A70` / night `#003F4F` grounds. Pearl El Messiri hooks 88–100px top-right. Tajawal body. The gold torn step tab, a soft CTA on a torn gold strip, a gold end slide with the pearl «الرابط في البايو.» tag. No faces, ever (hands, sleeves, backs of heads, feet, a hem, an abaya from behind). No words or numbers drawn on the art. No realism.

## Rules carried from QA (B1–B2)
- Big, simple, recognisable at a glance. If it reads as a mushroom or a muffin, redraw it.
- Soft CTA on a torn gold strip, small, low, never over art. Long slides: art smaller and lower; body never under 50px.
- Step tabs only for real labels («الخطوة الأولى:», «أول شي:», «الشي الثاني:», «ثالث شي:», «الضغطة السرية:», «قبل الكاشير:»). Check every tab slide by eye.
- No visible line ends with «،». The kit hides commas at breaks; fix breaks with keep_in / brk, never by changing words.
- End slide: gold ground, ink text, the post's motif small in a corner (vary the corner and pose per post).
