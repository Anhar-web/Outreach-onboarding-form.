### The headline
**The two reports land on the same lane from two directions.**
- The Audience Researcher found that the spark in every moment is **a line someone else says**: the teacher's "angel", the cousin's mum's "kids will be kids", grandma's "don't tell your mum".
- The Market Analyst found that **nobody debunks the social rules of the gathering**. Everyone debunks labels for the child ("stubborn", "attention-seeking"), and that is Stage 5 wallpaper in English.
- So the «الافتراض» series should name **the room's rule**, in a relative's mouth, never a theory about him or a belief of hers. Then a **ten-second tool that works in the room, in front of the witnesses**, and the laugh lands on her.

### What to stay away from (the Analyst's wallpaper list, condensed)
- **Overused calm-down lines:** «حافظي على هدوئك» (stay calm), «نظرات الناس لا تهم» (people's looks don't matter), "every child is different".
- **Overused reframes:** "not giving you a hard time, having a hard time", "connection-seeking", "tantrum vs meltdown" (also clinical for us), "name it to tame it", calm-down corners, emotion charts.
- **Overused relatives advice:** «اشكريها وتجاهلي» (thank her and ignore it), «وحّدي القواعد» (make the rules the same in both houses), the stock reply "this works for us".
- **The diagnosis trap:** every Arabic result on lining things up and picky eating ends in "is it a sign of…" or «استشيري طبيب» (see a doctor). We must never sound like that.
- **Tiredness signal:** there's a backlash against gentle parenting, while a trick like "Jessica" got 11M+ views. Mothers are tired of explanations and still want tricks.
