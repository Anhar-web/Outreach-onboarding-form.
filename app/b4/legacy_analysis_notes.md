### The shared skeleton (all 7)
- **s1 = the scene as a title + a list.** A short scene label («وقت الخروج من بيت الجدة.» / «قبل النوم بنص ساعة.» / «رحتي عزيمة. الناس جالسين») then **3 bulleted behaviours** (يرمي نفسه على الأرض / يصرخ / يرفض يلبس الشوز), then **witness lines in quotes** («خلاص خلوه شويه» «ليش معصب كذا؟»), then her position («وأنتِ واقفة عند الباب… تحاولي تلبسيه وسط الفوضى»). Not a question.
- **s2 = make it worse, beat by beat** («تحاولي ترفعيه… هو يتمدد على الأرض أكثر»), a judging line («واضح انه يحب العناد»), the punchline («وفجأة الرجعه للبيت صارت أكبر معركة في الزيارة»), then an open loop («ايش تسوي في هذا الموقف…»).
- **s3 = the reframe + ONE tool.** Fixed label: «الافتراض اللي يصعّب الموضوع:» (the assumption that makes it harder) → «لكن أحياناً…» (what it really means to him) → «جربي هذي:» + the tool name in bold + the exact words in quotes + what he'll do.
- **s4 = the twist/why in one line + engagement + CTA.** «الأطفال يحبون تصحيح الكبار. أحياناً بدل ما نحاول نقنع الطفل… نعطيه غلط يصححه.» Then an A/B "which one is your child" poll with emojis, or «اكتبي "حقيقي"». Then a soft CTA (link in bio / free sleep planner).
- **The solution lands on slide 3 of 4.** That's what Anhar is asking for in Batch 4.

### What the two big ones have that the 2–5K ones don't (first read; confirm with parts 2–5)
- **A universal, recognisable public/family moment** (leaving grandma's, the bedtime frenzy) vs. narrower ones (drawers, not talking, a prep checklist).
- **A counter-intuitive tool that sounds like a trick** (the wrong shoe, the "last push": tire him out *before* sleep) vs. sensible-but-expected tools (give him a task, a checklist).
- **A twist that flips an assumption** (kids love correcting adults; he needs to empty his energy *before* he calms down).
- **Saves and shares, not just views:** grandma's 1,707 saves and 1,106 shares; bedtime 227 and 193. People sent these to other mothers.
- Light, conversational humour in small doses («كأنه جاي يسوي تفتيش رسمي», «بنفس تركيز محقق في فيلم بوليسي», 🌪️ / 🔋 options).

### Notes against current rules (to weigh in the Batch 4 brief, not decided yet)
- The legacy s1 is a scene label + list + witnesses, not one question with a body/time cost. Both shapes have reached people; B3 family scenes worked with the question shape.
- Legacy posts used "we" lines («نعطيه غلط يصححه») and «كذا» in witness quotes; Batch 3 rules ban "we" for parenting moments (deceptive). Keep the B3 rule.
- Legacy used brain lines («دماغه يحاول يقاوم النوم», «دماغهم يترجمها») and emojis; current rules ban brain/clinical lines.

### What parts 1–2 now show (17 posts)
1. **The four big ones (203K, 159K, 39.5K, 38.4K) all name a moment almost every parent of a young child lives**: leaving a relative's house, the diaper change, chasing him in a restaurant, the bedtime frenzy. The 1–7K ones are narrower or more "my child specifically" (drawers, not talking, only listens when you shout, explodes after nursery, cries on waking).
2. **The biggest-ever hooks carry the same move as our B1–B2 winners: the cost lands on HER.** «…والمشكله؟ أنتِ الطرف الخاسر» (diaper, 159K) ≈ «وأنتي اللي نمتي» / «فصرتي تكنسي وهو نايم». The restaurant post: «وكأنكِ فريق إنقاذ… لوحدك», then «الإرهاق اللي يوصل للعظم».
3. **Same format, different results:** the diaper post (159K) and its siblings bath (5.6K) and car seat (3.7K) share the 3-slide cream format. The siblings lean on heavier performative jokes (hostage film, planking, "acid" water). The diaper post's humour is shorter and lands on her («ظهرك معوّج. صبرك طلع إجازة.»). Anhar's own note: the over-the-top humour "started to feel cringy". **Humour on her, light and one line at a time, works; humour that performs the child's drama doesn't.**
4. **The restaurant post has danger** (the table, the door to the street) and a reframe that lifts blame off her («مو لأنكِ ما تعرفي تسيطري عليه. لأن طفلك… لسه ما عنده "فرامل داخلية"»). 970 saves, 281 follows, and the comment prompt «اكتبي: "دوام مسائي"».
5. **Swipe depth is about the same for winners and the rest** (1.9–2.3 of 3–4). Reach came from slide 1 and topic, not from deep reads. That supports Anhar's "solution on slide 3" rule: most people never pass slide 2–3.
6. **Shares are the viral marker.** Diaper 1,598; grandma's 1,106; bedtime 193. Restaurant had 970 saves but only 2 shares (useful, but not "send this to my sister").
7. **The A/B "which one is your child" poll and «اكتبي "…"» prompts** appear on winners and losers alike, so they aren't the driver. Comments still depend on reach.
