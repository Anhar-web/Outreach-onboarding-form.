# Batch 4 custom-CTA method (Anhar, Nov 22)

**The question to ask before every CTA:** "What does she desperately want to be able to do differently in this situation, and how does taking the assessment bring her closer to it?" The assessment is the bridge to a solution that fits her child.

| Situation | What she actually wants |
|---|---|
| Bedtime | to get him to bed without the nightly struggle |
| Eating | more foods without every meal becoming a battle |
| Communication | help him express what he wants without both getting frustrated |
| Family visits | enjoy her family instead of managing one situation after another |
| Emotions | help him handle hard emotions and recover; calm him before it escalates into a scene |
| Transitions | move between activities without a meltdown |

## Her examples (the lines are hers; she flagged that the emotions one sounds translated)
- **Bedtime:** «تبغي وقت النوم يصير أهدأ بدل ما ينتهي كل يوم بنفس المعركة؟ عبي التقييم واكتشفي كيف تبدئي بخطوات تناسب طفلك.»
- **Eating:** «تبغي طفلك يتقبل أصناف أكل أكثر بدون ما تتحول كل وجبة لمعركة؟ ابدئي بالتقييم واكتشفي كيف تبني له طريقة تناسب تقبّله الحالي.»
- **Communication:** «تبغي تفهمي كيف تساعدي طفلك يطلب اللي يبغاه ويوصّل لك احتياجاته؟ عبي التقييم واكتشفي نقطة البداية المناسبة له.»
- **Family visits:** «تبغي تستمتعي بالعزيمة بدل ما تقضيها كلها ورا طفلك؟ عبي التقييم واكتشفي كيف تجهّزي طريقة تناسبه وتساعدكم تستمتعوا بالزيارة.»
- **Emotions:** «تبغي تساعدي طفلك يتعامل مع انفعالاته بدل ما تفضلي تطفّي نفس الحرائق كل يوم؟ ابدئي بالتقييم واكتشفي ايش يحتاج يتغيّر في طريقتكم.»
- **Transitions:** «تبغي الانتقال من اللعب للأكل، أو من البيت للسيارة، ما يصير معركة كل مرة؟ عبي التقييم واكتشفي كيف تبدئي بطريقة تناسب طفلك.»

## The journey
1. **The post** shows a problem she wants solved.
2. **The CTA** offers a relevant path toward solving it.
3. **The assessment** helps find what needs attention and where to begin.
4. **The landing page** shows how the Living Plan turns that starting point into a personalised, evolving home plan.

## Rules
- **Sell the solution.** Never claim the assessment itself makes him eat, sleep or stop melting down. It helps her find the right starting point toward that.
- **Invite her toward solving the problem**, not to "take an assessment".
- **Final check:** if she reads the CTA without knowing what the assessment is, does she still have a compelling reason to click? If not, it's still selling the assessment.
- **QR codes:**
  - Some posts' QR codes go straight to the landing page and skip the assessment.
  - A QR code for the assessment is fine even though the link is in the bio ("here's the QR, or the link in bio").
  - Tag every code by post.

## Tone rules (Anhar, Nov 22, evening)
- **Strong and certain.** No «يمكن», «ممكن», «لو حابة», «إذا تبغي», «جربي», «حاولي», «لو تقدري» in any CTA. Say what she gets, plainly.
- **Not homework.** Avoid «عبي التقييم», «جاوبي على الأسئلة», «خذي دقايق». Lead with what she walks away with («اعرفي من فين تبدئي مع طفلك», «خذي أول خطوة تناسبه»); the assessment is the vehicle, named second.

## Soft CTA decision (data, Nov 22)
- **B2:** 11 of 12 posts had a soft CTA on slides 3–5 → 9 completions. **B3:** all 12 have one → 0 completions so far (top reach 1.7K vs B2's 4.1K).
- **B1:** no CTA on the reach posts → 0 completions.
- So completions tracked **reach + having a CTA at all**. The soft CTA vs the final CTA can't be told apart. There's no evidence the soft line itself brought anyone.
- **Depth:** average 2.1–2.4 slides viewed of 7–8. The mid-post soft line sits right where people already leave, and it interrupts the value with an ad.
- **B4 decision (recommendation):**
  - Remove the assessment soft CTA.
  - On 7-slide posts, replace it with an honest "keep reading" line that promises what the last slides really give. The promise is the outcome she wants (the solution side), never "an assessment".
  - 5-slide posts need no bridge: the end is two swipes after the solution.
  - Test with/without the bridge line across the batch. The per-post tagged QR codes finally let us count scans per post.
- **Anhar's reason (the real rule):** a mid-post CTA asks her to commit to something before we've given her the value. **Give first. The only ask comes at the end.** No mid-post "follow", "comment" or "assessment". Any bridge line is a promise that asks nothing of her.
