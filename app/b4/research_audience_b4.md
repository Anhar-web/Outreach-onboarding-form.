## 3. Brief notes

**Real moments, not categories.**
- The line that starts it is spoken by someone else: the teacher's "angel", the cousin's "kids will be kids", grandma's "don't tell your mum".
- The witnesses at a Gulf gathering are a chorus: aunts, grandma, the giver, the host. The evil-eye line (#1) shows that guests and praise carry their own pressure.
- A stranger's kind line (#8) is rarer, and stronger as a final beat.

**Frustrations.**
- Her "no" cancelled in front of him (#12, #21, #22).
- Being judged as a mother for his manners (#2, #11, #23).
- The two-faced day: angel for others, storm for her (#3, #24).
- Her own temper, which she confesses only on anonymous consultation sites (#17–#19). That shame is real, so clear it with love lines and never quote it back at her.

**What builds trust.**
- Name the witness's line exactly.
- Let her laugh at herself (#26 is the coffee-shop model).
- Give a tool that works inside the moment.
- Show the moment from the giver's or aunt's side for a second (#31), without siding with them.

**What breaks trust.**
- The stock replies she's already heard ("this works for us", rehearse "thank you", stay calm).
- Picky-eating and lining-up content that ends in «استشيري طبيب» or "is it a sign of…". Every Arabic result for #7 and #10 does this. **We must not.**
- Clinical causes for the restraint collapse (the English articles name diagnoses).
- Blame («دلعتيه»).

**Biggest gap, and next step.** No Saudi witness lines were found in the wild. The best source is the comment sections on Anhar's legacy breakouts (grandma's 203K, restaurant 39.5K, wall post with 75 comments). Harvest those before step 7. Allowing alyaum.com, slaati.com, alukah.net, reddit.com and mumsnet.com in the network settings would let the next researcher read full threads.
