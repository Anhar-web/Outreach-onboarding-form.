## Part 2 · Batch 4 brief (DRAFT)
**Goal:** breakouts that send people via per-post QR codes to the landing page or the assessment. We measure scans, visits, starts, completions and waitlist joins per post (the Visits and Submissions tags), plus shares and saves.

**Audience:** a cold Saudi mother of a young autistic child who doesn't know Home Orbit. We describe behaviour she can see, never a diagnosis.

### Structure (Part O)
- **One tool, 5 slides:**
  1. Hook: pain plus her cost, with people reacting where possible.
  2. Deepen: observed one-liners, not performance.
  3. The tool.
  4. Proof: the method described.
  5. Twist (a confession at the end, coffee-shop model), then the CTA and QR code.
- **Three steps, 7 slides:** hook, deepen, step 1, step 2, step 3, proof, then twist + CTA + QR.
- **Give first:** no ask of any kind before the last slide. An optional "keep reading" promise line goes on 7-slide posts only, tested with and without.

### Topics
- **At least 8 of 12 are family or public scenes where people react with lines:** relatives, guests, the teacher, staff, strangers.
- **Universal battles plus 1–2 strange-but-common behaviours** (the wall-licking type).
- **A few legacy topics with a genuinely new moment or tool** (not re-runs); the rest are new.
- **Emotions and outbursts lead** (Q14: both Batch 3 completions, and most overall). **1 bedtime post.**
- **No repeats** of Batch 1–3 topics.

### Voice and humour
- Conversational, extreme love, funny but never performed.
- One observed detail per line.
- The cost lands on her.
- Banned: «طبيعي», "we", brain lines, emojis as bullets.
- All Feedback & Lessons rules apply.

### CTA (cta_method.md)
- Lead with the outcome she wants in this situation, stated with certainty, never as homework.
- The assessment is the bridge, never the promise.
- Run the "reads it without knowing what the assessment is" test on every CTA.

**QR codes (one per post, `src=qr&post=b4pN`):**
- **Recommendation:** 6 point to the matching `?focus=` landing page and 6 to the assessment, so we can compare the two routes.
- Send the bio link to `?src=bio`.
