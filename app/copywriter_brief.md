## The post shape (every post)
- **Slide 1 = the approved hook, VERBATIM** (copy it character for character from concepts.json, including her spelling and the double space). Nothing else on slide 1.
- **Slide 2 escalates the problem**: a beat-by-beat scene or her inner voice. It must not start the list. It should be the emotional peak (her Batch 1 peaks were all scene/inner-voice lines). Real-sized drama, no manufactured drama.
- **Soft CTA by slide 3 or right after the first step**: ONE short line at the bottom of that slide (mark it with `[soft CTA]` at the start of the line so design can set it small). It must feel like a natural aside, not a lead grab, and it must differ from post to post. Example spirit: «وإذا هذا يتكرر في أكثر من وقت في يومه، التقييم المجاني في البايو يساعدك تعرفي من فين تبدئي.» Don't copy that line.
- **The value**: the steps from the concept, said her way. Steps labelled in full («الخطوة الأولى:», «الخطوة الثانية:»…). Non-step items name what they are («أول شي:», «الحساب الأول:»). Give the example whenever a step mentions "the same words" or "a sentence". Every step must work today in a busy Saudi home, take seconds or use what she already has, and never be generic (no timers, "be patient", "make a routine", headphones, "warn him before the vacuum", the chair step-back).
- **The format label shapes the flow** (see concepts.json `angle` and the formats below). Let the idea find its shape; don't force a template.
- **~7 slides** (6–8 is fine). Few words per slide, at most ~4 short lines. No walls of text.
- **Proof (F1 posts only, one slide, can be merged with the CTA slide)**: the METHOD in plain words, only what's true: we look at his whole day, not just this moment; one moment at a time; the plan changes with how he responds, reviewed every two weeks; built from what she notices. No testimonials, no client case, no numbers, no "proven", no listing the six areas.
- **Final slide = the CTA**, following Anhar's formula **Connect → Purpose → Direct**, using the variation assigned in concepts.json (Observation / Reflection / Direct), written fresh for this post and tied back to its hook. It ends with «الرابط في البايو.» One CTA slide only; never repeat the CTA on a separate slide.
- **Caption**: line 1 = the hook's feeling + a one-line CTA (many people read the caption without swiping). Then 1–2 short lines. No hashtag walls (max 3 plain Arabic hashtags, optional). No emoji.
- **Pinned comment = "part two"**: one extra usable step not in the slides, then one plain line pointing to the free assessment in the bio.

## Formats
- F1 HPSPC: Hook → Problem (made worse, layers deep into the human "why", not cringe) → Solution steps → Proof (method) → CTA.
- F2 Story: one moment beat by beat (what he does, what she does, what she thinks) → the turn → the move she can make → CTA.
- F3 Two voices: two "voices" talk across slides (e.g. her that night vs her now; him at the door vs her inside) → the turn → the move → CTA. Mark speakers clearly in the text itself, naturally, not with labels like "Voice 1".
- F4 Metaphor: an everyday comparison carries the explanation (never a clinical/brain/battery metaphor) → what it means for her today → CTA.
- F5 Walk-through: hook as her desired result + "here are my 3 best…" feel (but slide 1 is the approved hook verbatim, so the promise goes on slide 2 or 3) → numbered points, one per slide, each with a TWIST that is unexpected but true and breaks advice she's heard → fast energetic close → CTA.
- F6 Checklist: a surprising take → a 3-part check revealed one part per slide, building tension so she checks her own life → the turn → CTA (the assessment as the full version of the check). Observations of moments, never "signs"; never reads like a screening.

## Hard rules (a violation = the post fails)
- Never: autism, any diagnosis, "sensory", "exposure", therapy, specialists, doctors, medical causes, promised results for the child, "independent sleeper" promises, timelines, labels on the child (عنيد، مدلّع…), mother-blaming, mocking her, the client case («يصحى معصب»), homework or quiz framing (log, write down, answer these), cups/plates in change posts, invented testimonials, numbers we don't have, scarcity.
- Arabic: no em dash (—) anywhere; no هال- contractions; no translated punctuation lists; no forced humour; don't open a scene slide with «و»; spoken lines in straight quotes "…" not «…»; تبدئي / ابدئي (never تبدأي); تسوي (not تسوين); ايش، فين، شي، أنتي، دحين، ما في؛ مبسوط؛ فيكي؛ إلين ما؛ وقت النوم؛ تلاحظيها؛ خطوات (not مواقف) for things she does; ما negates verbs, مو negates descriptions; يسحب for pulling a hand. Use «حاولي» when a tip depends on things outside her control; «كذا» to show why a tip works; «طبيعي…» to normalise his reaction; quote her inner voice; the mistake first, then what to do. Prefer the everyday detail over a cute invented one. Hand her value, don't order her as if she doesn't know what she's doing.
- Don't normalise spelling. Keep the hook's spellings (تنوميه، اثنين، ثمانيه، عشره، الاستشوار، صالون الاطفال، جمبه/جنبه).
- Read every line aloud as a Saudi mother to her sister. If it needs a second to decode, it was translated: rewrite from scratch.
