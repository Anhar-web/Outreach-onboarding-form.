## On your question 4: does a separate look at the assessment → waitlist step help the agency's goal?
**Not for this agency, not now.** The agency can do two things for the waitlist, and both are already in the plan:

1. **Reach the right mother.** More breakouts bring more assessments, at about 1 per ~1,100–1,250 breakout views.
2. **Arrive with the right expectations and beliefs.**
   - Every CTA now describes the assessment accurately, so she isn't disappointed on the results page.
   - Every post's explainer extension plants one belief the results page then confirms. The assessment's own core message is «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي طريقة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم», so the posts and the results will say the same thing.

The rest happens on the results and Living Plan pages, which the posts can't change. With 9 completions, the 0 waitlist joins aren't enough data to diagnose yet. **When to revisit:** if Batch 3 brings ~25–30 completions and the waitlist is still near 0, that's a page problem, and it's the job of your **conversion agency** skill (built for the Living Plan page), not this one.
