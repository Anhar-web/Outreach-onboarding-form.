## Market Analyst: Batch 3 (meltdowns · public & family scenes · body care)

*Method: the marketing-sophistication skill (map → stage → gap → match-check). It stops before hooks, which are the Hook Specialist's job. This builds on `app/research_market.md` (B1–B2) and doesn't repeat it. The B1–B2 findings still hold: Stage 5 heart, Lane A "the workarounds", Lane B "it starts before the moment".*

*Evidence: web sources are linked at the end. TikTok and Instagram pages still can't be fetched from this session. Account-level patterns are marked **observed**. Quotes in «» come from real Arabic articles, not our copy.*

**Where we stand overall:** we already speak at Stage 5 (her identity, her cost) and deliver Stage 3 substance (a sequenced method). Batch 3 keeps that split. What changes per bucket is **where the substance has to come from**, because each bucket's "tips" layer is worn out to a different degree.

### 1. Meltdowns and emotions (5 posts)
*When something ends, breaks or goes wrong, or when he hears "no".*

#### Stage: market Stage 5 in English, Stage 4 in Arabic. Her heart is at Stage 5.
- **English:** both the claim and the mechanisms are saturated.
  - Big Little Feelings: "make room for feelings while still keeping boundaries", "validate the feeling".
  - Sesame Workshop's script: "I'll help when your body is feeling calmer."
  - Co-regulation ("offer your calm presence"), "breathe together", "repair later", "practice calming skills when calm, not mid-meltdown".
  - The autism-specific layer (Child Mind, Children's National, Rula) is also saturated: visual schedules, countdown warnings, a consistent transition cue, "offer two or three choices", "reduce demands".
  - Even "practice before the meltdown" is already a named tip. Prevention in general is not an open lane. Only *her specific before* is.
- **Arabic:** Sayidaty, Mobtada, Al-Ahram, Alyaum, Foochia, Medela KSA, Verke and others all repeat the same moves:
  - «الإصغاء دون مقاطعة» (listen without interrupting)
  - «نظرة حانية أو لمسة على الكتف أو حضن دافئ» (a kind look, a touch on the shoulder or a warm hug)
  - «الرد بكلمات هادئة وتأجيل النقاش» (reply calmly and leave the talk for later)
  - «توجيه الطفل إلى الرسم أو الرياضة» (steer him to drawing or sport)
  - «لا تدخلي في مفاوضات طويلة» (don't get into long negotiations)

  Alyaum says «تجاهل النوبات» (ignore the tantrums) while Sayidaty warns against ignoring. Even the mechanisms argue with each other, which is a Stage-4 sign.
- **Wallpaper (do not lead with these, do not offer them as "the step"):**
  - validate / name the feeling («سمّي مشاعره», «احتوي مشاعره»)
  - stay calm («حافظي على هدوئك», «لا تعصبين»)
  - count to 10 / breathe together
  - calm-down corner
  - "5-minute warning" / countdown
  - visual schedule / timer
  - "offer two choices"
  - "ignore the tantrum"
  - "don't give in"
  - "talk about it after"
  - distraction («اسأليه عن نكهة العصير», ask him which juice flavour)
  - "first/then"
  - hug it out

#### Our level vs the market
- Our winners never opened on *his* feeling. They opened on what *she* has rearranged or what *she* pays.
- This bucket is where the B1 losers lived («يبكي وما تعرفي ايش يبغى؟», he cries and you don't know what he wants?).
- **The danger is format-driven:** an F7 "mistakes" list about meltdowns naturally drifts into "don't shout / don't ignore / don't negotiate", which is exactly the Arabic article list.

#### The open lane: "the second before it ends"
- Competitors work *during* the meltdown, or give generic "warn him first". Nobody shows her the tiny, specific moment just before:
  - the biscuit she broke in half for him
  - the cartoon she switched off mid-episode
  - "no" said while holding the thing he wanted
- Nobody shows what *she* already does in that second without noticing, either. That's her cost:
  - she waits at the door already in her abaya
  - she hides the iPad charger
  - she pre-breaks nothing and buys two of everything
- **Lane =** her own pre-emptive workarounds (Lane A) plus one concrete before-move she does in seconds, where she already is, that makes the end *his* (he picks the last thing, he does the switch-off, he holds the "later").
- Shown, never claimed. No "unlike other advice".

#### Mechanism to lead with (it's crowded)
- **What she notices just before is information, and the plan is built from it and changes as he responds.**
- In post terms: the last step of the value naturally becomes "and the next time you notice what came right before it…" → الخطة الحية is built from those notes.
- Don't name a "method", don't explain why he melts down (no "regulation", "nervous system", "overwhelm"), don't promise "fewer meltdowns".

#### Explainer types and beliefs
| Best types | Belief | How to carry it inside the rules |
|---|---|---|
| **6 Prevention Before Intervention** | **3** (what happens before matters) | The last step moves earlier in time: "and before the cartoon ends next time…". No "triggers". |
| **1 Hidden Pattern** | **1** (a reason I haven't recognised) | One everyday detail: the moment came right after the car, the hungry hour, the guests leaving. Never a cause claim, always "notice if…". |
| **2 Same Behaviour, Different Reasons** | **7** (same strategy won't fit every child) | Two boys, same broken-biscuit scream: one wanted it whole, one wanted to break it himself. Situational only. This is the safe way to do type 5 without "everyone tells you". |
| 4 The Parent's Knowledge | **4** (what I see daily is information, not a complaint) | "You already know which 'no' sets him off. That's not a complaint, it's where the plan starts." No «أخصائي». |

### 2. Public and family scenes with witnesses (4 posts)
*A loud women's party, the supermarket checkout, leaving a gathering, a restaurant-type scene.*

#### Stage: the tips are Stage 3 in Arabic and Stage 4 in English. The *witness* angle is Stage 1–2. Her heart is at Stage 5, the rawest of the three buckets.
- **English:** NPR's "tricky etiquette of parenting in public", Nurtured First, Parent Club, Romper and others.
  - Prevention (snack, shop after lunch, a set route avoiding the sweets aisle)
  - In-the-moment (leave or find a quieter spot, simple choices, a hug)
  - Onlookers: "you don't owe strangers an explanation", "skip the apology", "you'll probably never see them again"
  - Autism-specific: "remove from triggers", "reduce demands"
- **Arabic** (Al Jazeera, Sayidaty, Youm7, Supermama, Foochia, Shahen):
  - «توقفي تماماً عن عقاب الطفل والصراخ عليه أمام الضيوف» (stop punishing him or shouting at him in front of guests completely)
  - «اسحبيه جانباً» (take him aside)
  - «عدم الاستجابة لرغباته» (don't give in to what he wants)
  - «تخصيص لعب الطفل… في مكان قريب» (keep his toys close by)
  - «شرح الموقف عند العودة إلى المنزل» (explain it to him once you're home)
  - «التواصل البصري» (eye contact)

  Sayidaty's headline «لماذا يبدو الطفل مثالياً مع الناس.. ويتحول إلى مشاغب مع الأم؟» (why does a child seem perfect with others but turn naughty with his mother?) shows the genre is about *his* behaviour. Hers isn't the subject.
- **What nobody speaks to:** the witnesses' actual lines in Saudi family life. The aunt's «خليه شوي» (leave him a bit), the grandmother's «دلعتيه» (you spoiled him), «ليش زعلان؟» (why's he upset?), the women at the party turning round, the cashier waiting, the husband in the car honking. Also what she does to *avoid* being seen:
  - leaving before dessert
  - sitting by the door
  - shopping at 11 pm
  - saying «تعبان» (he's tired) for him

  In Arabic this is close to Stage 1 (observed: searches return articles, no Saudi-dialect creator owns it).
- **Proof it's open:** all three of Anhar's legacy winners (200K / 40K / 38K) were witness scenes with witnesses' lines, and the topic hasn't been run since the agency started.
- **Wallpaper:**
  - stay calm / don't shout in front of people
  - "don't care what people think" / "you don't owe them an explanation" (an empowerment line, now generic)
  - take him aside / to a quiet place
  - pack a snack
  - shop at a good time
  - don't give in to the candy
  - "talk about it at home"
  - noise-cancelling headphones at parties
  - "leave early"

#### Our level vs the market
- We're ahead: we already open on her scene and her cost.
- The risk is moving to "*what to say to people*" scripts. That's the empowerment genre and Stage 3.

#### The open lane: "the room is watching, so give him a job, not a lecture"
- She is the only one in the scene who has to act while being judged. Competitors give her a mindset ("ignore them") or an exit ("leave").
- **Lane =** one clever, game-like tool used *in front of* the witnesses that turns the scene. The child corrects, wins or has a role, so the witnesses see a game instead of a battle.
- It's paired with a prep move she does in seconds *before* entering (the car, the door, the trolley).
- This is the legacy wrong-shoe DNA, and F8 is literally built for it.

#### Mechanism to lead with
- **The tool works because of what she set up before the door, and that before is unique to him.**
- The explainer extension: "and what you noticed at the last gathering (which minute it started, who was holding him) is what the plan starts from."
- Don't explain why crowds are hard for him. No sensory or noise language.

#### Explainer types and beliefs
| Best types | Belief | How to carry it |
|---|---|---|
| **6 Prevention Before Intervention** | **3** | The tool is set up in the car or at the door: "before you open the door…". |
| **3 Real-Life Demonstration** | **8** (growth fits ordinary routines) | The checkout queue *is* the practice. No home drills required. |
| **9 Inside الخطة الحية** (light touch) | **11** (one starting point, not everything) | "Gatherings, the checkout, the party: the plan starts with the one that weighs most." The Q14 option "going out and family visits" makes the CTA line «تعرفي من فين تبدئي مع الطلعات والزيارات» (know where to start with outings and visits) accurate. |
| 4 The Parent's Knowledge | **5** (carry it without «أخصائي») | "Nobody at that party knows him the way you do. It just isn't written down yet." |

### 3. Body care and sensory chores (3 posts)
*Nails, teeth, new clothes, a hand dryer. Not a repeat of vacuum, blender, hair dryer, shower, hair wash or salon.*

#### Stage: English Stage 4 (crowded mechanisms, all OT-flavoured). Arabic Stage 2 (generic hygiene articles). As a *mother's cost* topic it's Stage 1. Her heart is at Stage 5.
- **English nails:** NHS OT leaflets (West Suffolk, Wirral, Northumbria, Betsi Cadwaladr), Your Kid's Table, Autism Parenting Magazine.
  - "after a warm bath when nails are soft"
  - "a fixed routine, e.g. Tuesday after bath"
  - "massage with lotion, vibrating massager, therapy brush"
  - "firm, maintained pressure"
  - "try filing"
  - "don't cut too short"
  - "count to 10, we stop at 10"
  - "trim a few every night while he sleeps" (the sleep workaround, sold as a solution)
- **English teeth:** Colgate, Delta Dental, Peaceful Parent.
  - "let him brush yours first"
  - "two brushes"
  - brush a stuffed animal
  - "chase the dirty puppy out"
  - dance party / song / timer / app
  - sticker chart
  - "start with 10 seconds"
- **Arabic:** Mawdoo3 and Colgate-AR.
  - «قصها بعد الاستحمام» (cut them after a bath)
  - «مقص أظافر خاص بالأطفال» (child nail scissors)
  - «فرشاة أسنان جديدة ومرحة» (a new, fun toothbrush)
  - «مدة التفريش دقيقتين» (brush for two minutes)

  These are instructional hygiene pieces with no mother in them.
- **Wallpaper:**
  - after the bath
  - while asleep
  - file instead of cut
  - massage first
  - count to 10
  - fun toothbrush / app / song / 2-minute timer
  - sticker chart
  - let him brush yours
  - cut the tags / seamless socks
  - "wash new clothes first"
  - headphones for the hand dryer
  - any "desensitise" / "sensory diet" / "deep pressure" language
- **Note:** "trim while he sleeps" is the *same* irony as our vacuum winner (an OT-sheet solution = her workaround). That makes nails the strongest Lane A hook in the bucket.

#### Our level vs the market
- Our winners in this bucket were hair wash (4.1K) and shower (3.2K). It's our proven home turf.
- We win on her cost (her back, her timing, the 3-person hold) plus seconds-long steps.

#### The open lane: "he holds the tool"
- Competitors make the *adult* the operator and add prep (massage, brush, timer).
- **Lane =** hand him a role so the chore becomes his move:
  - he picks the finger
  - he catches the mistake
  - he presses the button
  - he "checks" her work

  It takes seconds, in the bathroom she's already in, no kit.
- Paired with her cost (the night-time nail clipping, the 3-person hold, the hidden clippers) and with how it changes as he gets used to it.

#### Mechanism to lead with
- **The plan changes with him:** today he holds the clippers, next time he picks the order, later she cuts two in a row.
- That *is* belief 10 shown in miniature, with no interval named («كل أسبوعين» banned). Say «لما يتعود… الخطوة الجاية» (when he gets used to it… the next step).

#### Explainer types and beliefs
| Best types | Belief | How to carry it |
|---|---|---|
| **8 The Living Plan Evolves** | **10** (the plan must change with him) | Steps sequenced by *his* response, not by days. |
| **3 Real-Life Demonstration** | **8** | Done in the bathroom, at the sink, at the door. |
| **7 From Observation to Plan** | **12** (my observations can become a plan) | "Which finger he lets you do first, and which he pulls back: that's already a note." |
| 2 Same Behaviour, Different Reasons | **7** | Two children pull their hand away: one hates the click, one hates you holding the finger. Everyday words only. |

### 4. F7 Inception list: which bucket gets which framing

- **Mistakes → meltdowns** (1 post) and **public scenes** (1 post).
  - These buckets are already *full* of advice, so "the mistake most of us make" breaks the pattern.
  - **Hard rule:** every mistake must be a natural, loving move *she* makes in the moment, **never** a piece of popular advice labelled wrong (that would be type 5 done as "everyone tells you X", which is banned).
- **Benefits / one tool → body care** (1 post) and **public scenes** (1 post).
  - Concrete tools are saveable, and body-care posts earn saves.

**Candidate mistakes (meltdowns / endings / "no")**
1. **Announcing the end with the end already in your hand.** You hold his shoes or the remote, then say «يلا خلصنا» (come on, we're done). He sees the ending before he hears it. The move: let him do the last thing himself (press off, close the lid). *Not generic.*
2. **Fixing the broken thing out of his sight.** You rush the broken biscuit or crumpled drawing to the kitchen to "make it better", and he loses the thing *and* the say. The move: fix it with him, or let him decide «نرميه ولا نكمّله؟» (do we throw it or finish it?). *Not generic.*
3. **Saying "no" while still holding what he asked for.** The "no" and the object arrive together. The move: put it out of sight first, then answer, in two seconds. *Not generic.*
4. **Remembering only the scream, not the minute before it.** The explainer point (type 6 / belief 3): the minute before is the note the plan is built from.
- ⚠️ *Flagged as wallpaper, don't use:* "explaining too much mid-meltdown", "shouting back", "giving in", "ignoring", "saying 'it's nothing / don't cry'" (= "validate", generic).

**Candidate mistakes (public / family)**
1. **Answering the aunt instead of him.** Your first words go to the room («تعبان» / «ما نام», he's tired / he didn't sleep), and he hears you leave him. *Not generic.*
2. **The scramble at the door.** You hunt for shoes and the bag while he's already on the floor. The move: shoes go by the door at the start of the visit and the bag is packed before tea. *Not generic.*
3. **Staying for "just five more minutes" because someone asked.** The ending you planned gets taken by the room. *Realistic. Careful it doesn't blame her.*
- ⚠️ *Flagged:* "don't care what people think", "take him to a quiet room", "don't apologise to strangers" (all wallpaper).

**Candidate benefits / one tool (body care)**
1. **"He picks the finger":** he chooses which nail goes first and which is last. One choice, two seconds, and the last nail is his to name. *Not generic.*
2. **"Count the passengers":** each nail is a passenger getting off the bus; she miscounts, he corrects. *Fine. Make sure it doesn't read as "count to 10", which is wallpaper.*
3. **"New clothes meet the old":** the new shirt sits folded on top of his favourite one overnight. In the morning he chooses which goes on top. *Borderline: "wash first / let him touch it" is common. Use only with the choosing twist.*
4. **"He presses the dryer, you jump":** in a mall bathroom he pushes the hand-dryer button and her job is to be startled. *Not generic. Works for public too.*
- ⚠️ *Flagged:* after-the-bath, file-don't-cut, massage first, cut the tags, toothbrush app/song (all wallpaper).

**Candidate benefits / one tool (public)**
1. **The trolley job:** he's in charge of handing every item to the cashier, so the checkout becomes his job, not the wait.
2. **The exit signal:** two squeezes of her hand = he wants out, and she must answer it (even with «طيب، بعد القهوة» "okay, after the coffee"). *Not generic.*
3. **Shoes by the door at the start:** he puts his own shoes "in the parking" when you arrive, and parks them out when you leave.

### 5. F8 Scene + one clever tool + twist: candidates (wrong-shoe spirit)

All of these work the same way: the child corrects, wins or holds a role. She does it in seconds, where she already is. Witnesses see a game. **Don't reuse the wrong shoe itself** (legacy).

1. **Checkout: "you're faster than the cashier."** At the checkout he races the cashier and puts every item on the belt before she scans it. He wins every round.
   - *Twist:* the hard part was never the candy, it was standing still with nothing to do. Give him the cashier's speed to beat.
2. **Leaving the gathering: "you choose the last one."** Ten minutes before, she says «اختار آخر شي تسويه هنا» (pick the last thing you'll do here), and he chooses: one more slide, one more biscuit, say bye to the cat.
   - *Twist:* leaving is hard when the end gets taken from him. When he picks the last thing, he's the one who finishes.
3. **The loud women's party: "the secret button."** In the car, they agree that two squeezes of her hand means "I want out", and she always answers it, even with «بعد القهوة».
   - *Twist:* he doesn't need to scream to be heard; he already has a button. The women only see a hand squeeze.
4. **Teeth at the sink: "wrong place."** She brushes his nose, his ear, his elbow. He laughs, «لا! هنا!» (no! here!), and opens his mouth to show her.
   - *Twist:* same as the shoe. Kids love correcting grown-ups, so give him a mistake to fix instead of a mouth to open.
   - English "let him brush yours" is common; the *wrong-place correction* isn't (observed). Body-care bucket, but it carries a family witness line, e.g. grandma's «خليه، بكرة يفرّش» (leave him, he'll brush tomorrow).
5. **Restaurant wait: "take our order."** He's the waiter. Mum orders «شاي بالكاتشب» (tea with ketchup), he corrects her and writes or draws it on a napkin.
   - *Twist:* the waiting is the hard part, so he gets the busiest job at the table.
6. **Hand dryer in the mall bathroom: "scare mama."** He presses the button and her job is to jump.
   - *Twist:* the noise he starts himself isn't the same as the noise that catches him by surprise. Say it like that, in everyday words, with no "sensory".

**One-word comment prompts** in the spirit of «مناوبة مسائية» (evening shift), for the Hook Specialist to write: a word that names *her* role in the scene (the security guard, the cashier, the referee). Not the child's.

### 6. Rules check for everything above
- No «أخصائي», no «كل أسبوعين», no "everyone tells you X but…", no clinical or brain reasons, no "sensory", no promise of fewer meltdowns or of timing.
- Type 5 only through type 2 (same behaviour, different reasons), shown in one child's moment.
- CTAs claim only what is on `assessment_truth.md`. For public scenes, «الطلعات والزيارات» (outings and visits) is a real Q14 option. For meltdowns, «العصبية والانفجار» (anger and outbursts) and «الانتقال من نشاط لنشاط / إيقاف شي يحبه» (moving from one activity to another / stopping something he loves) are real options. Body care isn't one of the options, so keep its CTA general.

---

### Top recommendations for the Hook Specialist
1. **Meltdowns: open on her pre-emptive workaround or the second before**, never on his feeling. Examples: hiding the charger, buying two of everything, standing at the door in her abaya. Anything about "validate / stay calm / name it" is invisible on this feed.
2. **Public scenes: put a witness's actual line in the hook or on slide 2** (the aunt, the grandmother, the cashier, the women at the party). Nobody in Arabic owns this, and it's the DNA of the 200K legacy post. Keep the cost on HER (body or time detail).
3. **Nails is the strongest body-care hook:** "cutting them while he sleeps" is our vacuum-irony shape, and English OT sheets sell it as the solution.
4. **F7 mistakes = her loving natural moves** (announcing the end with the shoes in hand, answering the aunt first, saying "no" while holding it). Never popular advice framed as wrong.
5. **F8 tools must let the child correct or win** (wrong place, race the cashier, he picks the last one, the secret button, scare mama). The twist goes in everyday words.
6. **Carry the mechanism as a continuation, not a claim:** "what you noticed right before" → the plan starts there → it changes as he gets used to it. Types 6, 3 and 8 do most of the work. Beliefs 3, 8 and 10 are the batch's spine.
7. **Avoid every wallpaper term listed above**, including the empowerment lines ("don't care what people think") that feel fresh but are Stage 3 in Arabic.
