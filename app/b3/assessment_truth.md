# What the free assessment actually does (CTA truth sheet, Batch 3)

Source: Anhar's assessment blueprint «ليش نفس المواقف تتكرر مع طفلك؟» (uploaded Nov 16, 2026). Every soft CTA, final CTA, caption and pinned comment must only claim what is on this sheet.

## What it is
- A free assessment with 15 questions. Her answers produce a personal result.
- It looks at **her current approach** to the situations that keep repeating, not at the child. Three dimensions, each scored out of 12:
  1. فهم الأنماط المتكررة: what happens before a situation, preparing for it, what helps him calm down.
  2. تخصيص الطريقة لطفلك: fitting advice to her child, how he communicates, everyday practice.
  3. وضوح الخطة واستمراريتها: a repeatable approach, keeping progress going, other caregivers, fitting the family's routine.
- **She picks ONE situation** (Q14): sleep / going out and family visits / communication / food / moving from one activity to another or stopping something he loves / anger and outbursts / something else.
- She writes, in her own words, the change she wants to see (Q15).

## What she gets (true claims we may make)
- The strengths in her current way of handling things («نقاط القوة في طريقتك الحالية»).
- Her strongest foundation, and her next opportunity (what she needs to organise).
- **Where to start** («تعرفي من فين تبدئي»): the result has a «من فين تبدأي؟» section and a practical starting point.
- A starting point for **the situation she chooses**, plus **one small first step** she can try within everyday life.
- The change she wrote, shown back to her as her starting point.
- Afterwards, an invitation to الخطة الحية. It's optional and never required to see her result.
- The core realisation: «انتي مو محتاجة تجمعي نصائح أكثر. تحتاجي طريقة واضحة تناسب طفلك وحياتكم.»

## What it does NOT do (never claim)
- It does **not** look at "his whole day" or "the rest of his day" (يومه كامل / باقي يومه / يمر على باقي يومه).
- It does **not** cover "six areas of his day". There are six situations to *choose one from*.
- It does **not** solve this post's problem, explain **why** he does it, or find the cause.
- It does **not** assess or measure the child, and it doesn't diagnose.
- It does **not** give a full plan. That's الخطة الحية.
- It does **not** "show you what keeps repeating" for him. It shows her where her approach is strong and what to organise next.
- It does **not** promise the change she wants will happen, or by when.

## Safe CTA building blocks (her voice, accurate)
- «التقييم المجاني في البايو يساعدك تعرفي من فين تبدئي.» (her default line, accurate)
- «…ويساعدك تشوفي نقاط القوة في طريقتك، وايش تحتاجي تنظمي.»
- «…تختاري الموقف اللي يتكرر معاكي، وتطلعي بأول خطوة تناسبه.»
- «…تعرفي من فين تبدئي مع [this post's situation]». Accurate **only** when the situation is one of the six Q14 options (e.g. outings/visits, transitions, anger/outbursts, food, communication). Otherwise she can enter it as "something else", so keep the line general.

## Batch 2 CTA lines that overclaimed (don't reuse)
- «التقييم المجاني في البايو يمر على باقي يومه» (hair wash): it doesn't go over his day.
- «يساعدك تشوفي يومه كامل» (night waking, B1 cries): it doesn't look at his whole day.
- «يساعدك تشوفي ايش اللي يتكرر» (salon): it looks at her approach, not his patterns.
- B1 / handbook Part E «التقييم المجاني يمر على ستة جوانب من يومه»: there are six situations to pick one from, not six areas reviewed.
