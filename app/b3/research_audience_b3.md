### 2. Moments by bucket

The **cost on HER** is the hook material. **Seeds** are tool ideas only. They take seconds, happen where she already is, and work with what he wants. The copywriters write the Arabic.

#### Bucket 1 · Emotions / meltdowns (10 candidates)

**E1. The lift button**
- **Scene:** Someone else presses the lift button (mall, hospital car park, their building), or the doors close before he presses. He screams, drops to the floor, and hits the panel.
- **Cost on her:** She rides **back down to the ground floor with the shopping bags on her wrists** so he can press it himself. Or she waits for an empty lift while others go up.
- **Witnesses:** Strangers in the lift, and the neighbour who pressed.
- **What she's tried:** Explaining; holding his hands. Both fail.
- **Seed:** Before the doors open, give him the job out loud: «انت مسؤول الأزرار اليوم». He gets his job before the trigger.

**E2. The broken biscuit or banana**
- **Scene:** The biscuit snaps, the banana breaks, or she peeled it "wrong". He wants a whole new one and won't take this one.
- **Cost on her:** **She eats the broken halves herself**; her snack is always his leftovers. Or she opens the last packet and has none for later.
- **What she's tried:** "It's the same thing" doesn't work; giving a new one every time does.
- **Seed:** Break it on purpose, as a game: «نسوي له عملية؟ صار ثنتين!». The break becomes his choice.
- ⚠️ Keep it about the *break*, not about food variety (his plate and 3 foods are banned).

**E3. A sibling takes his toy, or knocks his tower over**
- **Scene:** His little brother grabs the car, or knocks down what he built. He screams and hits.
- **Cost on her:** She **sits on the floor between them for the whole afternoon** as referee. Or she has started **buying two of every toy**.
- **What she's tried:** "Share" fails; punishing makes it worse.
- **Seed:** A turn token. He holds an object (a spoon, a cap) meaning "you're next", so waiting has something in his hand.

**E4. Losing a game**
- **Scene:** Uno, a race to the door, a board game with cousins. He loses and throws the cards or screams «غش!».
- **Cost on her:** She has **lost every game on purpose for months**. Older siblings complain «ليش دايم هو يفوز؟». She has stopped bringing out family games.
- **What she's tried:** Letting him win, then refusing to. Both blow up.
- **Seed:** She loses *first* and *loudly*, modelling it in five seconds: «خسرت! يلا مرة ثانية». Or they play "us against the timer" so nobody loses to him.

**E5. The plan changes**
- **Scene:** She promised grandma's house or the playland. Dad's late, or it's closed. Meltdown in the car.
- **Cost on her:** She has **stopped telling him any plan until they're at the door**, and carries the whole week's plans silently in her head.
- **Seed:** A «يمكن» word. Every plan is said as «يمكن نروح، وإذا ما صار نسوي…», with the backup named the same second.

**E6. "No" at home**
- **Scene:** He hears «لا» at the fridge, at the front door, or to the sweets on the high shelf. He drops to the floor.
- **Cost on her:** She has **stopped saying «لا»**. She hides biscuits on top of the wardrobe and opens the fridge only when he's not looking.
- **What she's tried:** Explaining, then giving in.
- **Seed:** "Yes, when": «إيه، بعد الغدا». The same limit, with no «لا» to hit.

**E7. Waiting**
- **Scene:** For the food at a restaurant, for Dad in the car outside the shop, for the bath to fill.
- **Cost on her:** She **orders his food before she sits down** and eats hers cold, or boxed in the car.
- **Seed:** Give him a waiting job: count the people coming in, or "be the waiter" and take orders on a napkin.

**E8. Something ends at the mall: the coin ride, the trampoline zone, the ice cream**
- **Scene:** The ride stops.
- **Cost on her:** She **feeds it coins six times**. Now she **exits the mall through the other door** so he won't see the rides.
- **Witnesses:** The queue of kids waiting.
- **Seed:** A "last ride" ritual that he controls: he presses "the end" (taps the ride twice and says bye).

**E9. A toy breaks**
- **Scene:** A wheel comes off his car. He wants it fixed *now*.
- **Cost on her:** She's **at the kitchen table with glue at 11 pm**.
- **Seed:** A "toy hospital" box. Broken toys go in, with a time named for "tomorrow's surgery". It's realistic but weaker.

**E10. A balloon pops, the bubbles run out, a sandcastle collapses**
- These are classic "ends or breaks" moments, but low on Saudi specificity. Keep as reserve.

#### Bucket 2 · Public and family scenes with witnesses (9 candidates)

**P1. The women's wedding hall (قاعة)**
- **Scene:** The زفة music starts at full volume. He covers his ears, screams, and pulls her toward the door.
- **Cost on her:** She spent **two hours on makeup and dress**, then **sits in the lobby or bathroom corridor all night**, or in the car with the driver. She **leaves before the dinner**, which is often served after midnight. She's seen by everyone she knows.
- **Witnesses:** The relatives' lines [REPO, not found verbatim]: «خليه شوي ويتعود», «ليش جايبته؟». The invitation card's «جنة الأطفال منازلهم» line is the social pressure (#20–21).
- **What she's tried:** Leaving him home, which isn't always possible. Sitting in a separate room the whole night (#19 [PARA]).
- **Seed:** "The last song." Before the زفة, he picks the signal: «بعد هذي الأغنية نطلع للممر نرتاح ونرجع». The break is planned and his, not an escape.

**P2. The weekly family gathering (جمعة, at grandma's or the in-laws')**
- **Scene:** A cousin takes his toy, or the dallah or dates go to someone else first. He screams in the majlis.
- **Cost on her:** She **spends the whole evening in the side room or hallway** with him, eats last, and **hears the comments through the door**.
- **Witnesses:** «حمولتي تنتقد» (#1), «دلعتيه», «اضربيه» (#2 [PARA]), «الرجال ما يبكي».
- **Seed:** A host job. He hands out the dates or the tissues, so the gathering has a role for him.
- **Watch out:** **Not leaving and not shoes.** The legacy post owns that.

**P3. The checkout candy wall**
- **Scene:** «لا» to the sweets at the till. He's on the floor, the queue is watching, the cashier waits.
- **Cost on her:** She **pays for the sweet he already opened**. Or she **leaves the full trolley** and carries him out (Mumsnet 5373610 [EN]).
- **Witnesses:** The people in the queue, the cashier's look, an older woman's «عطيه وخلصينا».
- **Seed:** "The belt boss." From the aisle, his job is to put every item on the belt and count them, so his hands are full before the sweet wall.

**P4. The restaurant: waiting for the food (not the legacy chase)**
- **Scene:** Twenty minutes for the order. Screaming, the next table turning around.
- **Cost on her:** She **eats in the car**, or **asks for her plate in a box** before it arrives.
- **Seed:** The waiter game (E7) or "count the plates coming out".

**P5. Mall rides and the toy-shop exit (E8 in public)**
- The twist «صرتي تطلعي من باب ثاني؟» is strong. The witnesses are the other mothers and the ride attendant.

**P6. Waiting rooms (not clinics)**
- **Scene:** The airport gate before a summer trip, a جوازات or bank queue, the car service centre.
- **Cost on her:** She **walks the terminal for two hours** with the stroller.
- **Witnesses:** Strangers' looks; «ليش يصيح؟».
- **Seed:** A pocket "waiting bag" with one surprise item, opened only in queues.
- ⚠️ This is less of an everyday moment, so keep it as reserve.

**P7. The lift full of strangers (E1 in public)**
- The witnesses actually *see* her ride back down. This works as an F8 public scene too.

**P8. A relative's birthday party**
- **Scene:** Another child blows out the candles. He screams «أنا!».
- **Cost on her:** She **takes him out before the cake is cut** and misses the photos.
- **Witnesses:** «عيد ميلاد بنت خالتك، مو لك».

**P9. Eid door-to-door visits**
- **Scene:** «سلّم على خالتك», he refuses or hides.
- **Cost on her:** She **apologises at every door**.
- **Watch out:** It's off-season (Nov), and the aunt figure is banned. Keep for later.

#### Bucket 3 · Sensory chores and body care (9 candidates)

**B1. Nail cutting**
- **Scene:** He screams and pulls his hand away. Holding him takes two adults (HealthTap [EN]).
- **Cost on her:** She **cuts his nails while he's asleep, by phone torch, crouched by his bed, one hand a night**. He wakes after three nails, and the rest wait till tomorrow (Mumsnet [EN, PARA]). That is a vivid body and time detail.
- **What she's tried:** Holding him down, cutting while he sleeps, the clippers after the bath.
- **Seed:** "Your turn first." He "cuts" (touches the clipper to) her nail, then she does one of his, and they count one each.
- ⚠️ Frame it on the nails, **not on bedtime** (no sleep posts).

**B2. Hand dryer or toilet flush in the mall bathroom**
- **Scene:** The dryer blasts. He screams, won't go in, and drags her out with wet hands.
- **Cost on her:** She **holds his ears with both hands and skips washing her own**. She **holds it all outing**, or **checks which mall bathrooms have paper towels** (the Mumsnet "our toilets" map [EN]).
- **Witnesses:** Women at the sinks, who sometimes press the dryer on purpose to "help".
- **Seed:** He's the "button boss". He decides when, on «٣، ٢، ١», with his hands over his own ears. Or they flush after he's out of the stall.

**B3. The winter jacket (timely: it's November)**
- **Scene:** Riyadh or Qassim mornings are cold. He fights the sleeves and screams, «يحكني!».
- **Cost on her:** She **carries his jacket over her arm on every outing**, and wears it on her shoulders herself.
- **Witnesses:** Grandma: «بيبرد الولد!».
- **Seed:** The "jacket flip". She lays it on the floor upside down, he puts his arms in and flips it over his head. He does it himself, in five seconds.

**B4. New clothes and labels (wedding or Eid outfit)**
- **Scene:** He refuses the new thobe or outfit and wants the faded T-shirt.
- **Cost on her:** She **washes the same shirt every night** so it's dry by morning, **cuts every label off** and buys the same shirt in three sizes. At the wedding he wears the old one and everyone asks.
- **Witnesses:** «ليش ما لبّستيه الجديد؟»
- **Seed:** The new piece goes on under or over the old favourite for one minute at home first. Pairs with #25.

**B5. Teeth brushing**
- **Scene:** Lips clamped, head turned away.
- **Cost on her:** She **brushes on the bathroom floor with him between her knees**, ten seconds and done. Or she has quietly dropped the night brush.
- **Seed:** "Find the food" game: «وين الرز؟ لقيته!». He opens to show.
- ⚠️ The night brush is close to bedtime, so frame it as morning or after meals.

**B6. Wiping his face or nose (winter colds)**
- **Scene:** He screams at the tissue. At the gathering his face is sticky or his nose is running.
- **Cost on her:** Relatives notice, and she **chases him round the majlis with a wet wipe**.
- **Seed:** A mirror. He wipes it himself, looking at himself in her phone camera.

**B7. Changing clothes after a spill**
- **Scene:** Juice down his shirt. He refuses to take it off.
- **Cost on her:** He **stays wet and sticky for the whole visit**, and she hears «غيّري له!».
- **Seed:** Two shirts held up: «الأزرق ولا الأحمر؟». The choice is which shirt, not whether to change.

**B8. Combing hair (daughters)**
- **Scene:** She screams at the brush every morning.
- **Cost on her:** She **sends her with loose, messy hair**, and grandma comments «شعرها منكوش».
- **Seed:** She holds a hair section herself.
- ⚠️ It's close to hair wash, so keep it as reserve.

**B9. Sunscreen**
- This is real in English (#36), but weak for Saudi realism in November and for indoor kids. Reserve only.

### 4. Gaps and caveats
- **There is no verbatim Arabic for the witnesses' lines** «خليه شوي», «ليش زعلان», «دلعتيه», «الرجال ما يبكي». These come from Anhar's legacy post [REPO] or from paraphrase. The only verbatim witness advice found is «خليه يبكي بيرجع يسكت» (#5, quoted by an educator). **The best source is still the comment sections on Anhar's own posts.**
- **There is no Gulf mother's quote for any body-care moment:** nails, teeth, hand dryer, jacket, face. Those moments rest on English parent threads, so the Saudi wording is untested. Copywriters must not present seeds as quotes.
