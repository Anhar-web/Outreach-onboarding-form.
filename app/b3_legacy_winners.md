# Anhar's legacy winners (before the agency). Batch 3 input, Nov 15 2026
Her summary. The format stopped working because she repeated it over and over, but when it worked it got great results.

## 1. Leaving grandma's house: best post ever (200K+ views, 2K+ likes, hundreds of new followers)
- Scene: time to leave grandma's house. He throws himself on the floor, screams, refuses his shoes. Everyone stares. People say "it's okay, just leave him a bit" / "why is he so upset?". You're trying to get his shoes on in the middle of it.
- Deepen it: you try again, he lies on the floor; you try again, he pulls his leg away. Going home is now a bigger battle than the outing itself.
- Reframe: the shoes mean the fun is over.
- One tool, "the wrong shoe": take his shoe, say "I think this is mine" and try to put it on. He corrects you ("no, this is mine!") and usually puts it on himself.
- Twist (Brendan Kane): kids love correcting adults. Instead of convincing him, give him a mistake to correct. Clever, like a game, fun.

## 2. Restaurant chase / inner brakes (~40K views, likes ~993 to confirm, 281 new followers, lots of comments)
- Scene: in a restaurant, running after him before he climbs on a table or opens the door to the street. A whole rescue team on your own.
- Reframe: the running, the worry, the looks, the bone-deep exhaustion aren't because you can't control a child. He doesn't have inner brakes yet.
- Prevention (train it at home instead of a daily chase): the signal game (walk together; green go, yellow slow, red stop; every day) plus a pillow track ("how do we get past this safely?") so he thinks before he moves.
- Explanation: some kids don't feel danger quickly; they go for the movement before the result.
- Close: "if you feel like a full-time security guard, write: evening shift". Lots of people commented "evening shift". Funny, but not over the top.

## 3. Bedtime energy (38K+ views, 354 likes, 53 new followers)
- Scene: half an hour before bed he runs, laughs out loud, jumps on the bed. Someone says "he's obviously not sleepy."
- Deepen it: you try to calm him down; he cries, screams, refuses his pyjamas. Bedtime is a mess.
- One tool, "the last push": "before bed we have a challenge": a few minutes of jumping, running, pushing something, then straight into the routine.
- Twist: sometimes a child doesn't need to calm down first. He needs to empty his energy, then he calms down.
- Anhar: we've done a lot of bedtime lately; change things up.
