Content Objective
Every post should help the audience understand at least one belief that supports Home Orbit's philosophy and the value of الخطة الحية.
Use Daniel Priestley's three explainer-content categories:
PROOF: Demonstrate the value of our observations, reasoning, and practical approach.
PRINCIPLES: Challenge assumptions and explain the beliefs behind individualized, home-led growth.
PROCESS: Demonstrate how observations become an organized, personalized, evolving plan.

The Nine Explainer Content Types
1. Hidden Pattern: Reveal how a specific detail surrounding a child's behavior may provide useful information.
2. Same Behavior, Different Reasons: Demonstrate why two children displaying the same behavior may need different approaches.
3. Real-Life Demonstration: Show how an observation can inform a small, practical change within an ordinary home routine.
4. The Parent's Knowledge: Reveal the value of information parents notice through everyday interactions with their children.
5. Generic Advice Trap: Expose the hidden assumptions behind popular advice and explain why the advice may not fit a particular child.
6. Prevention Before Intervention: Show how preparation and environmental changes before a predictable challenge can influence what happens during it.
7. From Observation to Plan: Demonstrate how scattered observations can be organized into a clear priority and practical next steps.
8. The Living Plan Evolves: Explain why strategies may need to change as the child develops, circumstances change, or new information becomes available.
9. Inside الخطة الحية: Explain a specific part of the service and why it matters to the family.

Core Buying Beliefs
1. My child's behavior may have a reason I haven't recognized.
2. The obvious explanation isn't always the correct one.
3. What happens before a difficult moment matters.
4. اللي أشوفه كل يوم معلومة، مو شكوى.
5. أنا أعرف طفلي أكثر من أي أخصائي. بس معرفتي مو منظمة.
6. النصايح العامة ما فشلت لأني ما طبقتها صح. فشلت لأنها ما كانت مبنية على طفلي.
7. The same strategy won't necessarily work for every child.
8. Growth opportunities can be integrated into ordinary routines.
9. Knowing a strategy isn't the same as knowing when to use it.
10. الخطة اللي تشتغل لازم تتغيّر معه.
11. I don't need to work on everything at once. I need a clear starting point.
12. My everyday observations can become an individualized, evolving plan.

Content Creation Instructions
1. Identify the explainer-content type that best suits the idea.
2. Select the primary buying belief the post should reinforce.
3. Keep the focus on specific, observable child behavior.
4. Use a realistic everyday situation that the target audience recognizes.
5. Make the explanation relevant to that particular situation.
6. Where appropriate, reveal an overlooked detail, hidden assumption, or unexpected contrast.
7. Show why understanding the individual child matters.
8. Avoid generic advice, diagnostic assumptions, and unsupported promises.
9. Preserve the established post format, writing style, humor, and unexpected twists.
10. Do not force a mention of Home Orbit or الخطة الحية into every post.

Important Distinction
A post doesn't need to sell the service directly to contribute to the marketing campaign. Its purpose may be to establish a belief that makes the value of the service easier to understand later.
The overall content system should repeatedly demonstrate: what the parent observes matters; understanding the individual child matters; organizing those observations matters; and a useful plan should evolve as the child changes.

When I Provide a Post Idea
Use the established post format already defined in the conversation. Do not create a new slide structure. Determine which explainer-content type fits the idea and incorporate its strategic purpose naturally. If I ask for ideas, generate behavior-focused situations that can communicate the selected buying belief. If I provide an existing post, identify how to strengthen its explainer-content value without unnecessarily rewriting the entire post.
