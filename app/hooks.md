## Safety flags

- **3.2 (name vs fridge): borderline.** "Doesn't respond to name" is on every «علامات» list. Slides must frame it as a home check only, include a plain "get his hearing checked" line, never mention signs, diagnosis or what a result "means". If the team is unsure, swap to 3.6.
- **2.6 (postponing the iPad): borderline, mother-blame risk.** It can read as "you give in". Keep only if the next slide immediately says this is what every mother does and moves to the check. Otherwise drop (1.3 or 3.3 cover the topic).
- **2.4 (ears/vacuum):** describe only. No "sensory" cause, no ear-defender recommendation, no hearing claims.
- **2.3 (nuggets):** humor must stay on her memory, never on his eating. If he is losing weight, the post needs a "see a doctor" line.
- **1.4 (park):** critiques the 5-minute warning as incomplete, not as wrong; keep that tone on the slides.
- **2.2 / 1.5 (bedtime):** never promise he'll sleep alone or through the night; promise a lighter evening for her.
