# Market Analysis: Home Orbit social batch (10 carousels)

Builds on `agency-run/market.md` and `positioning.md` (landing page: Stage 3 mechanism market with a Stage 5 emotional heart). This pass looks at the **content feed**: what Arabic/Gulf parenting and special-needs content already tells a cold mother, and where a value post can still stand out.

Method note: web search does not index TikTok and Instagram posts directly. The evidence below comes from the Arabic web layer the feed recycles (sayidaty, mawdoo3, webteb, asharq, aljazeera, al-ain, alkafeel forums, ajel), English autism-parent sources and TikTok studies, plus the prior research.md. Each tip type below appeared in at least 3 independent Arabic sources.

---

## 1. Sophistication stage of the content topic

**Stage 3 for tips, close to Stage 4 for the tip formats themselves. The audience's heart is Stage 5.**

The cold mother's feed has already given her every generic *claim* ("your child can be calmer, sleep, eat") and most generic *methods*. Content that is now wallpaper:

| Wallpaper type | Evidence | Why it no longer lands |
|---|---|---|
| **"علامات التوحد" lists** (eye contact, name response, flapping, lining up toys) | asharq "11 علامة تؤكد إصابة طفلك بالتوحد", sayidaty x3, mawdoo3, alkhaleej | Off-limits for us anyway. It also frightens her: arabicpost describes new mothers asking groups about *every* behavior. |
| **"٧/١٠ نصائح لنوبات الغضب"**: stay calm, distract, don't give in, praise good behavior, time-out | masrawy "7 نصائح", al-ain, mawdoo3, alkafeel, aljazeera "دليل شامل", asharq/Mayo | Identical lists everywhere. They assume a typical toddler tantrum and add a "see a specialist if it's over 25 minutes" line. |
| **Sleep routine**: fixed bedtime, warm bath, dim lights, under 30 minutes | mawdoo3 x2, asharq "7 نصائح", sayidaty, arabi21 | Every one is about *the bed*. None looks at the day before it. |
| **Picky eating**: don't force, involve him in cooking, keep re-offering, reward | sayidaty x3, alarabiya, hespress | Written for a typical picky eater. It doesn't fit a child who eats 5 foods. |
| **Diet/cure claims**: GFCF, «علاج التوحد بالغذاء», "81% improvement" | mawdoo3, webteb x2; SMJ review: 4 of 9 studies show no effect | Hope followed by let-down (the pattern in market.md). Banned for us. |
| **Screen-time fear**: «التوحد الافتراضي», «لا للشاشات», screens cause speech delay | ajnet, eremnews, webteb, youm7 (Autism Day 2026), al-ain, snrt | Mostly guilt. It implies she caused it. |
| **Transitions**: give a 5-minute warning, use a timer, don't negotiate, carry him out | English: Time Timer, PBS, Sesame; Arabic: generic tantrum lists | She has tried it. Even the vendors admit "a single timer isn't enough". |
| **Generic reassurance**: «كوني صبورة», «كل طفل مختلف», «النتائج تحتاج وقت» | balagh, mumzworld, maspero | Comforting, but nothing she can use. |
| **Behavior-modification jargon**: تعزيز إيجابي/سلبي، إطفاء، عقاب مستقطع | mawdoo3, cura.sa, maspero | Clinical, and it frames the child as something to correct. |

The English feed is one step ahead, at **Stage 4**. It sells competing mechanisms: low-demand parenting (a viral TikTok with about 489k views), "meltdown vs tantrum" explainers (understood.org and others), visual timers, sensory diets. A TikTok study found only 27% of #autism videos were accurate. English parents complain about "generic advice without listening" and note that "nuance does not translate to short-form".

**What's still almost absent in Arabic:** content that asks the mother to *observe something across her child's day and use what she sees*. English sources ask parents to log "what happened right before" and note that stress "builds for hours". In Arabic this barely appears, apart from one aside in a mawdoo3 article ("فكر بما يحدث قبل السلوك وبعده"). No Arabic content gives the mother a concrete check she runs on her own child.

## 2. This batch's level vs the market's

- **Generic tip content (the market's default):** Stage 2 claims ("10 tips so he calms down") delivered in a Stage 1 format (a list). It is pitched below where the audience is, so it reads as wallpaper.
- **This batch must work at Stage 3, with Stage 5 openers.** Open with *her moment* (identification, no blame). The value has to be a *how* she hasn't seen: look at what happened earlier in his day, not only at the moment itself. That keeps us out of the "11th list" and the "علاج" thread, and it is the same mechanism the assessment and الخطة الحية actually use. There's no gap between what we post and what we sell.
- **Don't jump to Stage 4.** Don't argue "our method beats timers or diets". Saying "the timer didn't fail you, it only covers the last 5 minutes" is fine. Attacking other methods or specialists is not.

## 3. The open lane

**Everyone gives her answers about the moment. Nobody gives her a way to look at the day before the moment.**

Specifically, the open lane is **observation-based, one-moment value content in Gulf dialect**. Each post takes one exact repeating scene (taking the iPad away, the عزيمة at grandma's, bedtime, the plate he won't touch) and gives her **a check she runs on her own child**: 3 questions, a 2-day look-back, a "before" map. She comes out with *her* answer, not ours.

Three things make it open:
1. **Arabic content is answer-first.** Every post tells her what to do. None helps her see her own child's pattern.
2. **Gulf-specific moments are unserved.** العزايم, بيت الجدة, Eid gatherings, long family visits and late Gulf bedtimes are almost absent from content. The only results were English holiday guides and a UAE news piece.
3. **No blame.** The screen-guilt and "the mother is the key" framings leave room for a voice that says "you already see what no session sees".

## 4. What "usable value" must look like

To avoid reading as the 1000th tips list, a post should meet at least four of these:

1. **One exact moment, not a topic.** "The 10 minutes after you take the iPad away", not "transitions". "The first half hour at بيت الجدة", not "outings".
2. **Built on her observation.** The core of the post is something she *checks*: "for the next 3 times, note…", "think back to yesterday: did any of these happen?"
3. **Small and countable.** 3 questions, one 2-column note, one change to try. Nothing that needs a printer or a 30-day program.
4. **Whole-day logic shown, not stated.** Every check reaches back in time (yesterday's sleep, what came before, how full the day was), so the mechanism is *demonstrated* inside the free value.
5. **No promised outcome.** End with "you'll see what comes before it", not "he'll calm down".
6. **Saveable.** A last slide she can screenshot (the check itself), because saves drive carousel distribution.
7. **Describe the behavior, never label it.** No "sensory overload", "meltdown", "anxiety" as cause. Say «يغطي أذنه», «يرمي نفسه على الأرض», «ياكل نفس الأكل».

Wallpaper test: if you could swap the post onto sayidaty with a stock photo, rewrite it.

## 5. Mechanism to lead with, and the honest bridge

**Mechanism (plain words, no coined term):** «نشوف يومه كامل، مو الموقف وحده» → «مجال واحد في كل مرة» → «نعدّلها معك كل أسبوعين». In attention posts, show only the first piece, inside the check. In conversion posts, name the full chain once, on the second-to-last slide.

**Bridge from free tip to assessment.** The free check and the assessment are the same kind of looking at different sizes. That's what makes the bridge honest:
- Slides 1–6: one moment → her check → what she might notice.
- Bridge slide: «هذا موقف واحد من يومه. التقييم المجاني يشوف ٦ جوانب مع بعض: النوم، الطلعات والزيارات، التواصل، الأكل، الانتقال من شي يحبه، والمشاعر… عشان تعرفي من وين تبدئي.»
- Last slide: «الرابط في البايو. بعد التقييم، إذا حبيتي، فيه قائمة انتظار للخطة الحية: خطة تُبنى من يومه، مجال واحد، وتتعدل معك كل أسبوعين.»
- **No scarcity in cold posts.** No countdown, no «الأماكن محدودة». The true "10 mothers" line belongs on the landing page only. A cold mother who hasn't met the brand reads any scarcity as a sales trick.
- The assessment is the *natural next step of the check she just did*, not a gift box: "you looked at one moment. This looks at the whole picture."

## 6. Identity angle

Use it, and keep it light: **«انتي تشوفي اللي ما يشوفه أي تشيك لست.»** Every check post *proves* this. Her notes are the data. It replaces both «كوني صبورة» (which treats her as the problem) and «الأم مفتاح النجاح» (which puts pressure on her). For outings, identity carries the post: she's the one doing ألف حساب before every invitation. Name that, then give her a plan for the visit. Other people's judgment («مو متربي») may appear only as something said *to* her, at most once in the batch.

## 7. Topic recommendations (ranked by how open the lane is)

| # | Behavior + moment | The check/value | Lane | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The عزيمة / بيت الجدة visit**: the first half hour and the moment he's had enough | A "before we go" 3-question prep (what does he know about the place, where is his quiet corner, what's our exit sign) + an after-visit note: when did it turn? | **Widest open.** No Arabic content, highest emotional load | Attention (identity-led), also strong conversion |
| 2 | **Morning anger**: he wakes up angry most days | A 2-day look-back: last night's bedtime, how the evening ended, what's first on the morning's list. It's the case-1 mechanism shown, not told | Very open; content treats it only as an infant sleep issue | **Conversion** (whole-day proof) |
| 3 | **Taking the iPad away** / stopping something he loves | A "which stops are hard?" check: is it every stop or only some? What comes *after* the stop? Is it worse late in the day? | Open in angle (the timer tip is saturated; the "which and what comes after" angle is not) | **Conversion** |
| 4 | **The same 5 foods**: the plate he won't touch | Write his safe-food list and what they share (colour, crunch, brand, temperature). Keep them; the list is information, not failure | Open vs diet/force content; the anti-diet stance is credible | Attention |
| 5 | **Bedtime that starts at 4pm**: he can't settle at night | An evening-load check: what happened from Asr to bed (visits, screens, noise, a late nap)? Pick one to try moving | Semi-crowded (sleep tips); open only through the whole-day angle | **Conversion** |
| 6 | **Covering his ears / leaving loud places** (weddings, malls) | A "loud map" of his week: where, what time, how long before he covers his ears. Describe it; no "sensory" diagnosis | Open in Arabic; English covers it, Arabic covers it thinly | Attention |
| 7 | **The guessing moment**: he cries or pulls you and you don't know what he wants | A note: what was he near / looking at / doing just before? 3 times this week | Moderately open; speech content is all about words and screens | Attention (keep it complementary to speech therapy; no promise of words) |
| 8 | **The stares in public**: what to say to relatives | One short line to say + one exit plan | Emotionally open but thin on value unless built as a script | Attention (use sparingly) |

**Suggested split:** attention posts 1, 4, 6, 7 and one of 8 or a second outing post. Conversion posts 2, 3, 5, plus a conversion version of 1 and an "all six areas" self-check (which of the six moments repeats most this week?). The six-area self-check maps 1:1 onto the assessment and is the most honest bridge of all.

**Stay out of:** signs/checklists of any condition, meltdown-vs-tantrum explainers, screen-guilt, diet/supplement anything, "10 tips", reward-chart behavior modification, self-harm content (if it ever comes up, point to a doctor).
