## Market Analyst

*Method: the marketing-sophistication skill (map, then stage, then gap, then match-check), stopping before final hooks. This builds on agency-run/market.md, positioning.md and MESSAGING_GUIDE.md (Stage 3 market, Stage 5 heart). It updates that work for cold TikTok and a broader "hard daily moments" frame. Evidence is either sourced (links at the end) or marked "observed". Instagram/TikTok pages could not be fetched from this session, so the account-level claims below are observed patterns, not quotes.*

### 1. Sophistication stage per topic

**Bedtime: Stage 4 in English, Stage 3 in Arabic. Her heart is at Stage 5.**
- English: the *mechanism* is already common. "Graduated withdrawal" / "the disappearing chair" (sit beside the bed, then move toward the door over several nights) is in NHS handouts, Happiest Baby and Little Otter. Big Little Feelings sells a course whose sleep guide covers "laying with them to fall asleep" and making your child "an independent sleeper". Sleep consultants lead with a "free customized sleep evaluation" (Sleep Sense) or "1,530,425 parents took this sleep assessment" (Smart Sleep Coach).
- Arabic: Stage 3. MOH, Sayidaty, Altibbi and Nestlé all repeat the same routine-and-environment advice. No visible Saudi-dialect creator owns the "he only sleeps if I lie beside him" moment (observed: searches surfaced articles, not creators).
- Our Batch 1 winner already used the step-back method. That worked because it was framed through *her*, not because the method was new.
- **Wallpaper:**
  - «روتين ثابت قبل النوم» (a fixed bedtime routine)
  - «حمام دافي وقصة» (warm bath and a story)
  - «إضاءة خافتة» (dim lights)
  - «ابعدي الأجهزة قبل النوم بساعة» (no screens an hour before bed)
  - «دمية/دبدوب» (a comfort toy)
  - «تدريب النوم» (sleep training)
  - "the chair method"
  - "independent sleeper"
  - "sleeps through the night"
  - "customized sleep plan"

**Chores and noise around the child: Stage 1–2 as a *mother's* topic, Stage 3 as a "sensory" topic.**
- Advice on noise sensitivity exists, but only clinically: NHS audiology/OT sheets and Romper. Their tips:
  - count down before the sound
  - let him switch the vacuum on
  - give the vacuum a silly voice
  - gradual exposure
  - "vacuum when the child isn't around"
- Note that last one: what OT sheets offer as a *solution* is exactly the workaround our winning hook names ironically («فصرتي تكنسي وهو نايم؟»).
- Nobody frames this as *her housework and her rest*. In Arabic, this is close to Stage 1 (observed).
- **Wallpaper to avoid:**
  - "sensory" vocabulary: «حساسية سمعية» (sound sensitivity), «تحسس حسي» (sensory over-sensitivity)
  - «تعريض تدريجي» (gradual exposure, which reads as therapy)
  - «سماعات عازلة» (noise-cancelling headphones, the default tip)

**General toddler hard moments (tantrums, transitions, crying): Stage 5.**
- English: Big Little Feelings and the gentle-parenting field have saturated the space with "validate the feeling", "name it to tame it", "connection before correction", "big feelings" and "Big Feeler" quizzes.
- Arabic: "positive parenting" articles in Al Arabiya, Sayidaty and Asharq Al-Awsat. **Wallpaper:**
  - «التربية الإيجابية» (positive parenting)
  - «احتوي مشاعره» (contain his feelings)
  - «سمّي مشاعره» (name his feelings)
  - «لا تصرخي» (don't shout)
  - «كوني قدوة» (be a role model)
  - «تجاهلي الخطأ وامدحي الصواب» (ignore the bad, praise the good)
  - «حددي قواعد واضحة» (set clear rules)
  - «اصبري» (be patient)
  - «١٠ نصائح» (10 tips)
- She has heard every claim and every mechanism. She trusts only being seen.

### 2. Batch 1 vs the market

- **The two winners** opened at Stage 5 with an identity move. They named an adaptation *she* had made («تكنسي وهو نايم», «وأنتي اللي نمتي»), a moment no competitor speaks to. Then they delivered Stage 3 value: concrete steps that worked like a method.
  - The irony let any mother of a young child recognise herself (a wide audience on TikTok's test).
  - The steps earned the saves (36 on bedtime).
- **The eight stalled** because they opened at Stage 2–3. Each hook was the child's behaviour plus "here's how to handle it", for example:
  - «تسحبي الآيباد ويبدأ الصراخ؟» (you take the iPad and the screaming starts?)
  - «يبكي وما تعرفي ايش يبغى؟» (he cries and you don't know what he wants?)

  Every parenting account opens this way, so on a cold feed it reads as one more tip post. Behaviour-first openers also sound narrower and more clinical, so TikTok's first ~450 viewers didn't stop.
- The conversion posts also asked for the click *before* they had earned trust, and on a slide nobody reached.

### 3. The open lane(s)

**Lane A, "the workarounds" (the main lane).**
- Every competitor talks about the child's behaviour or the parent's technique. **Nobody names the life she has quietly rearranged around him**:
  - vacuuming at night
  - lying down until he sleeps
  - cooking him a separate plate
  - leaving the gathering early
  - whispering in her own house
  - showering only when he's asleep
- Each post opens on one workaround, framed with a true, gentle irony about her. Then it gives steps that undo *that one workaround*, a little at a time.
- This is a Stage-5 voice (identity) carrying Stage-3 substance (method), and it is the proven hook shape.

**Lane B, "it starts before the moment" (the bridge lane).**
- Competitors fix the moment itself: bedtime at the bed, the vacuum when it's on.
- We show that the hard moment is shaped earlier in his day: the hour before bed, what he was doing before the noise.
- Fair claim: we say "notice what came before", never "X causes Y".
- This lane *is* our mechanism (whole day, not the moment alone) and Anhar's own bedtime CTA. It hands the click a reason.

### 4. The mechanism to lead with

- **In her terms:** «خطة للبيت مبنية من اللي تلاحظيه أنتي في يومه. تبدأ بموقف واحد، وتتعدل كل أسبوعين حسب ايش يصير.» (A plan for home, built from what you notice in his day. It starts with one moment and is adjusted every two weeks based on what happens.)
- **Don't lead with «خطة مخصصة»** ("a customized plan"). Every sleep consultant says it ("customized sleep evaluation"), so it is wallpaper. Lead with the two things they don't claim: **built from her observations** and **it changes with him**.
- **How to show it as «رؤوس أقلام» (key points), not an ad.** The post demonstrates the method instead of describing it:
  1. One step in the post is a noticing step, given as a fact rather than homework, e.g. "the hour before bed has three things that change how the night goes."
  2. The steps are sequenced ("start with one; after a few nights, the next"). That *is* "one area at a time" and "adjusts", shown in miniature.
  3. One proof line, no more, on the CTA slide or the slide before it. Example: «كذا نشتغل: نشوف يومه كامل، موقف واحد في كل مرة، ونعدّل كل أسبوعين.» (This is how we work: we look at his whole day, one moment at a time, and adjust every two weeks.)

  No feature list, no six areas, no testimonials.
- **Don't invent:** no "framework" name, no claims about the brain or senses, no "proven".

### 5. Identity angle

- **She is the one who has quietly adapted.** She's resourceful, not failing; tired, not careless.
- The irony is *with* her, never at her: "you found a way around it, and the way around it is costing you."
- The identity line to aim at: **"the mother who built her day around his hard moments, and is ready to build one back."**
- Universal enough for any toddler mother (the Generalist Principle), and true for the specific buyer.
- **Avoid:** "boy mom" or "gentle parent" identities (borrowed English camps), martyr framing, and any label on the child.

### 6. CTA lane

**What the market does:**
- label quizzes ("Is your child a Big Feeler?")
- big social-proof counters
- comment-a-keyword-for-a-DM (Instagram automation)
- "book a free call"

All of these read as lead capture, and we can't use label quizzes or invented numbers.

**Our advantage:** the assessment *is* Lane B. It looks at the rest of his day, so it can be the natural continuation of a post about "before the moment". Most viewers leave after slide 2, so:

1. **The open-loop soft CTA (slide 3, under the first step, small type):**
   - Connect: "this step is for bedtime."
   - Purpose: "what happens earlier in his day changes how it works."
   - Direct: «التقييم المجاني في البايو يساعدك تعرفي من فين تبدئي.» (The free assessment in the bio helps you know where to start.)
   - One line only. It is placed as a footnote, not an ask.
2. **"One workaround of many" (final slide, Reflection):**
   - Connect: name the workaround from the hook.
   - Purpose: "it's rarely the only one, and the plan starts with the one that weighs most."
   - Direct: the assessment shows where to start.
   - This ties back to the hook, the way p10's «بدل ما تخمني» did.
3. **"Notice this, then start here" (Observation):**
   - Connect: the one detail from this post to notice.
   - Purpose: "these details are what the plan is built from."
   - Direct: «ابدئي بالتقييم المجاني.» (Start with the free assessment.)
   - This is Anhar's bedtime model, and it is safest for F1 and F5.
4. **Caption line 1 = hook + one-line CTA.** Many viewers read the caption without swiping (observed platform behaviour).
5. **The pinned comment as "part two":** one extra step plus the assessment line, so a profile tap feels like following the content.

Never: "sign up", "spots limited", "take the quiz", or "find out if your child…".

### 7. Format recommendations

| Topic | Best formats | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Bedtime sequel** | **F5 Walk-through** (twist: "the step that matters most happens before bedtime"), **F2 Story** (the night told from her side, beat by beat) | F5's "unexpected but true" twist suits a Stage-4 topic where she knows the chair method. F2 carries the Stage-5 heart. |
| **Chores/noise sequel** | **F3 Two voices** (her inner voice «بكنس لما ينام» against the reality of 11pm), **F1 HPSPC** (seconds-long steps, method as proof) | The topic is low-sophistication, so a plain, direct problem → solution works. The inner voice was the proven emotional peak (p5 s2). |
| **General moments** (shower, separate plate, whispering, leaving early) | **F6 Checklist build** ("three workarounds you've built without noticing"), **F4 Metaphor** (e.g. her day arranged like a timetable around him; never a clinical metaphor) | Stage 5 needs self-reflection and tension. A checklist of *her* adaptations creates the "that's me" moment and leads naturally to the "where to start" CTA. |

Use F1 as the control on at least one sequel so the results can be compared.

### 8. Risks / what to avoid

- **Repeating the chair step-back as the main tip.** It is now generic (Stage 4) and it was our own last post. Sequels need a new set of steps (the hour before, the night wakings, the return line) or a new angle on the same steps.
- **Sensory and therapy drift on noise posts:** "sensory", "exposure", "desensitise", headphones as the fix, or any hearing or OT line.
- **Promises:** "sleeps alone", "independent sleeper", "calm". Name the goal, never the result or a timeline.
- **Irony that tips into mockery** of her, or into martyrdom.
- **Lead-grab mechanics:** label quizzes, comment-for-link, invented counts, urgency.
- **Over-describing the mechanism** (a brochure) or listing the six areas.
- **Borrowed English vocabulary** («تربية لطيفة» for gentle parenting, «مشاعر كبيرة» for big feelings). It reads translated and joins a crowded Stage-5 field.
- **Breadth risk:** universal hooks will draw mothers of typical toddlers. That's acceptable for reach, but the steps and CTA should hold for the mother with a harder day, without labelling him.

**Hook directions for the Hook Specialist (not final copy):**
- the workaround she's built
- the true cost of that workaround to her
- "it starts earlier than the moment"
